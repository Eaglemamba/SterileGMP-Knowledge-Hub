5.4.12 Guidance on Setting Minimum Incubation Times for Growth-Based Methods 生長型方法最短培養時間之設定指引

原文內容 Original Text

Growth-based methods require an appropriate incubation time to ensure the recovery of microorganisms in the presence of the test sample and under the conditions of the test method. Minimum incubation times may be established from the longest TTR (Time-to-Result) for challenge microorganisms determined during primary validation, method suitability, and/or comparability studies.

Adding a safety factor to the longest TTR should be considered. For example, rounding up to the nearest whole day can be a relatively simple way to add such a safety factor. For example, if the longest TTR for a slow-growing microorganism is 4.6 days, then the minimum incubation time for the routine test can be 5.0 days.

When appropriate, a larger safety factor may be considered, for example, an extra full day may be added to the longest TTR. Using the same 4.6 day length of the longest TTR for a slow-growing microorganism, the minimum incubation time for the routine test could be 5.6 days.

Both of these options are relatively simple to implement, and the stakeholder can justify a minimum TTR that is suitable for the test method and routine-use requirements.

Another approach in setting a safety factor is based on the time needed for a ten-fold increase in the amount of incubation time for the slowest-growing organism used during validation studies. This can be calculated by multiplying the estimated generation time in the test media by log2(10) as illustrated in the following equation:

T = tTTR + log2(10) · G

Where:

T = incubation time for microbial detection in the test sample to be examined (hours (h))
tTTR = longest TTR determined during primary validation, method suitability, and/or comparability studies (h)
G = generation time of slowest-growing microorganism (h), which may be determined by conducting separate studies or based on published literature

Using the same example, if the slowest microorganism has a TTR of 4.6 d (110.40 h) and has a generation time of 1 h, the minimum time of incubation would be 110.40 + 3.32 × 1 = 113.72 h (or 4.7 d). Similarly, if the slowest microorganism has a TTR of 4.6 d (110.40 h) but has a longer generation time of 5 h, the minimum time of incubation would be 110.40 + 3.32 × 5 d = 127 h (or 5.3 d).

Stakeholders who want to calculate their own generation times can inoculate relevant organisms in the alternative growth-based method and use the following equation:

G = t / [3.32 × log10(N / N0)]

Where:

t = time interval (h)
N = number of cells/CFU at the end of the time interval
N0 = number of cells/CFU at the beginning of the time interval

Note: Depending on the output signal measured, other formulas may apply.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

Time-to-Result / TTR (檢測所需時間): 在驗證研究（Primary Validation、Method Suitability、Comparability）中，每一株挑戰菌（Challenge Microorganism）被偵測到訊號所需的實際時間。「最長 TTR」通常來自生長最慢的菌株（例如黴菌、孢子形成菌）。

Generation Time / G (世代時間): 微生物數量倍增所需的時間，是計算培養時間的關鍵動力學參數，可由文獻取得，也可由實驗室自行以對數成長公式回推。

Safety Factor (安全係數): 在最長 TTR 之上額外加乘的緩衝時間，目的是確保日常放行測試（Routine Test）在真實生產條件下，仍有足夠時間偵測到極少數的慢生長微生物，避免偽陰性（False Negative）放行。

比喻說明 Analogy

設定最短培養時間就像規劃「最後一班高鐵」的發車時刻：如果統計上最慢的乘客（最慢生長的菌株）平均要 4.6 天才能趕到月台，你不會把發車時間精準卡在 4.6 天整——因為只要有一點延遲（批次差異、基質干擾），這班車就會「漏掉」真正的陽性訊號。因此無論用「無條件進位到整天」還是用世代時間的對數公式往後推算，本質上都是在幫最慢的乘客多留一點緩衝時間。

重點提示 Key Notes

公式 T = tTTR + log2(10) · G 中的 log2(10) ≈ 3.32，代表「族群數量增加十倍」所需的世代數。這個係數是刻意設計的保守值：它假設即使已經偵測到訊號，微生物數量仍需要再放大十倍才能被穩定、重複地判讀為陽性，因此必須把這段「訊號放大時間」也計入最短培養時間。世代時間 G 若取得不準確（例如用了非典型基質下量測的數值），會直接放大或縮小整個安全係數，因此建議在實際的 AMM/RMM 生長基質中重新量測 G，而非直接引用文獻的一般培養基數據。

公式與計算 Formula

方法一：無條件進位法（Rounding-up）
最長 TTR = 4.6 天 → 最短培養時間 = 5.0 天

方法二：加碼一整天法（Extra full day）
最長 TTR = 4.6 天 → 最短培養時間 = 5.6 天

方法三：世代時間換算法（Generation-time based）
T = t_TTR + log2(10) x G
範例 A：t_TTR = 110.40 h, G = 1 h
  T = 110.40 + 3.32 x 1 = 113.72 h ≈ 4.7 天
範例 B：t_TTR = 110.40 h, G = 5 h
  T = 110.40 + 3.32 x 5 = 127 h ≈ 5.3 天

反推世代時間：
G = t / [3.32 x log10(N / N0)]
（t：時間區間；N：區間終點菌數；N0：區間起點菌數）

實務應用 Practical Application

對於 CDMO 而言，最短培養時間直接牽動放行前置時間（Release Lead Time）。若一款 AMM/RMM 的最長 TTR 是 4.6 天，選擇「方法一」（5.0 天）而非「方法三」（4.7 天）看似更安全，卻可能讓客戶產品的放行時間多出好幾小時，在急迫的臨床批次交付時造成商業壓力。因此建議在驗證計畫（Validation Plan）階段就以風險評估決定安全係數策略，並與品保、客戶端 QA 事先溝通清楚，避免驗證完成後才發現培養時間設計過於保守，反而抵銷了導入快速方法的商業效益。

練習思考 Practice Questions

  1. 若你的實驗室測得某黴菌在 AMM/RMM 專用培養基中的世代時間為 8 小時，而最長 TTR 為 96 小時，請用世代時間換算法計算最短培養時間（小時與天數）。
  2. 為什麼不能只用「文獻上的標準世代時間」，而必須在自己的 AMM/RMM 生長基質中重新量測 G？
  3. 三種安全係數設定方法中，哪一種最適合用於快速放行（Rapid Release）情境？為什麼商業考量與科學考量在此可能出現張力？
5.4.13 Guidance on Changing Acceptance Criteria 可接受標準變更之指引

原文內容 Original Text

Though growth-based, CMM (Conventional Microbiological Methods) provide results in terms of a CFU, which could arise from a single cell or multiple cells. Some AMM/RMMs will provide results or signals that are different from the CFU (e.g., a viable cell count, a spectral analysis, relative light units, fluorescent units, etc.). As such, these AMM/RMMs could potentially produce superior results (e.g., higher quantitative counts) when compared with growth-based methods. Although strategies exist for demonstrating comparability between a non-CFU method and a CFU method, data derived from non-CFU methods may not align with conventional acceptance criteria, such as alert and action levels or specifications, because the number or rate of recovery in these methods may be higher (see Section 5.4.10). Accordingly, the implementation of these non-CFU methods may require the establishment of new acceptance criteria.

This is not a new concept. In a 2006 non-peer reviewed publication, the author's concluded that new microbiological methods rely on a completely different body of information—some may be direct measurements, some indirect—and that previous acceptance criteria may not be applicable. Therefore, implementation of newly developed, or more rapid microbiology methods may also require the establishment of new acceptance criteria (86).

The 2022 revision of EU GMP Annex 1 allows AMM/RMMs, instead of growth-based, CMMs, to be used for EM as long as they are appropriately validated and meet the intent of providing information during steps when potential for contamination of the product exists. More importantly, throughout the document, the microbiology limits are based on the CFU and, if new technologies are used that present results in a manner different from the CFU, the manufacturer should scientifically justify the limits applied and, where possible, correlate them to the CFU (9).

Similarly, the 2023 Swissmedicines Inspectorate publication Interpretation of GMP Annex 1 2022 (Rev. 1) addresses whether it is possible to fully replace microbiological monitoring using settle plates and volumetric air-sampling systems by other integrated sampling and testing systems, such as AMM/RMM. The document states that the equivalence of methods should be demonstrated, and the effectiveness of the chosen method should be proven. Additionally, for the use of real-time viable particle-counting and given the nonequivalence of autofluorescent unit versus CFUs and current GMP pharmacopeial limits are in CFUs, the company needs to collect data on their process for the real-time viable particle-counting to compare it to the standard EM data (87). However, consideration should be given to the environments in which these studies are performed, as some samples will provide microbial recoveries (e.g., Grade C and D, or controlled, non-classified areas) while other environments may not (e.g., Grade A and B areas), in which case alternative strategies may be used, including trending of zero to non-zero results. Additional guidance is provided in Section 11.0 (Appendix 3).

AMM/RMMs that do not provide results as a CFU signal, are not based on the growth of microorganisms, or are more sensitive than growth-based CMMs, may provide results that are numerically superior (higher) than the CMM being compared. For these reasons, acceptance criteria historically based on the CFU, such as EM alert and action levels or microbial specifications, may need to be changed to reflect the recovered counts using the non-CFU method.

Establishing a numerical relationship between a non-CFU signal method to a CFU method may be possible but only if the data can be correlated mathematically. For example, if the non-CFU signal is always five times greater than the CFU method numerical values, then a 5X correlation factor may be used to establish new acceptance levels. However, when a direct correlation between numerical values cannot be demonstrated across the practical range of the test, across different sample matrices, different microorganisms, or across different environmental areas, a general correlation may not be possible. In this case, setting new acceptance levels for the AMM/RMM should be considered.

Understanding the AMM/RMM's baseline measurements, similar to those described for comparability studies, may be useful in establishing new acceptance levels, as recoveries considered to be “normal” would not necessarily be treated as an OOE (Out-of-Expected) detection event. Where numerical baseline measurements indicate the normal background noise in the absence of a test sample, these measurements may be subtracted from the test sample measurements, when justified.

The most conservative approach is to continue to use the existing CFU levels and extend this to data derived from using the AMM/RMM. For example, if an AMM/RMM demonstrates a normal baseline for continuous EM as 0 CFU/m3, then excursions to this baseline may indicate an OOE event. Separately, if an AMM/RMM indicates occasional recovery events over the course of some monitored period of time, then these data should be used to establish the normally expected baseline. Using this information can help to establish new acceptance criteria that can be related to the historical control measures using a conventional CFU method. In this instance, the results from the AMM/RMM are used to make a quality decision about the test result. This aligns with the decision-equivalence concept described in USP <1223>.

Stakeholders may consider the following examples of how to establish new acceptance criteria:

  • Set the highest AMM/RMM recovery counts as an action level when you also show the corresponding CFU count is at an acceptable level.
  • Use 97.5% and 99.5% percentiles or 2 and 3 standard deviations from the mean-AMM/RMM counts for alert and action levels, respectively, based on the AMM/RMM historical data (i.e., baseline). This may also confirm correspondence to CFU acceptable levels.
  • Apply trending rules to determine when an out-of-trend (OOT) result is observed, when compared with historical (baseline) data that may include numerical values for recovered counts and/or the frequency of recovery or nonzero events over some period of time. This may be most appropriate for a large number of test samples or monitored environments that are expected to have no or very-low detection events. This may also be applied to higher detection events as long as the baseline is appropriately defined. OOT events may include the following:
    • One or more points outside the established alert or control limits
    • Six points in a row that are steadily increasing
    • Fourteen points in a row that are alternating up and down
    • An unusual or nonrandom pattern in the data, when compared with historical data
    • Any combination of the above recommendations

Discussions with relevant regulatory agencies or regulatory submissions may also be required prior to any changes being finalized or implemented.

Ultimately, any changes to established specifications must be related to fitness for use and be suitable for the application of interest. Additionally, final specifications should be scientifically sound, justified, and based on an appropriate risk analysis.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

non-CFU 訊號 (非菌落形成單位訊號): 部分 AMM/RMM 並非以「長出一個可見菌落」為判讀基礎，而是輸出活細胞計數、螢光強度（RLU）、光譜訊號等，其數值不必然等於 CMM 的 CFU，甚至可能因偵測到單一活細胞而非群落而數值偏高。

Decision-Equivalence (決策等效性): USP <1223> 提出的概念——不強求 AMM/RMM 的數值與 CMM 數值一致，而是要求兩者在「品質決策」上得出相同結論（例如都判定為合格/OOE），這是重新設定可接受標準的理論基礎。

OOE (Out-of-Expected) / OOT (Out-of-Trend): OOE 指單次結果超出預期基準；OOT 則是根據歷史趨勢（如連續上升、交替變化）判斷異常，兩者皆是 non-CFU 方法建立新管制邏輯的核心工具。

比喻說明 Analogy

這就像把「以公里計費」的傳統計程車，換成「以感應器偵測到的震動次數計費」的新型車隊。如果新車隊的感應器天生就比里程表敏感，直接套用舊的「每 10 公里收 300 元」標準會不合理——你必須先蒐集新車隊在正常路況下的震動次數基準值，才能訂出對應的新收費標準，而不是硬要它的數字跟舊計程車一模一樣。

重點提示 Key Notes

EU GMP Annex 1 (2022) 明確要求：若新技術的結果表現方式異於 CFU，廠商必須「科學上證明其限值合理」，並「盡可能」與 CFU 建立關聯（PIC/S Annex 1 2022, Section 8.5 相關精神）。這代表監管機關並未強制要求 1:1 數值換算，但要求企業必須能說明限值訂定的科學依據——這正是本節建議的基準值法、百分位數法、趨勢法存在的監管背景。務必注意：任何可接受標準的變更都可能需要與主管機關溝通或送審，不可僅憑內部決議逕行變更放行標準。

公式與計算 Formula

數值關聯法（Correlation Factor）：
若 non-CFU 訊號 = 5 x CFU 數值（於整個測試範圍內穩定成立）
→ 可用 5X 校正係數，將既有 CFU Alert/Action Level 換算為新方法的等效限值

百分位數法（Baseline Percentile）：
Alert Level  ≈ 97.5th percentile 或 平均值 + 2 SD（歷史基準資料）
Action Level ≈ 99.5th percentile 或 平均值 + 3 SD（歷史基準資料）

OOT 判定規則（任一成立即視為異常）：
- 1 點以上超出管制/警戒界線
- 連續 6 點單調遞增
- 連續 14 點交替升降
- 出現異於歷史資料的非隨機型態

實務應用 Practical Application

對於已導入即時螢光微粒計數器（Real-Time Viable Particle Counter）做環境監測的 CDMO，若直接把既有的 CFU 警戒/行動水準原封不動套用到新系統的自發螢光單位（AFU），極可能出現大量「假性 OOE」而觸發不必要的偏差調查，徒增品保與生產人員負擔。正確做法是先蒐集新系統至少數個月的基準資料（尤其是 Grade C/D 或非分級管制區，因為 Grade A/B 幾乎沒有背景訊號可供統計），再依本節建議的百分位數法或趨勢法重新訂定限值，並將整個訂定邏輯文件化，以備稽核與法規溝通之用。

5.4.14 Ongoing Maintenance and Periodic Reviews 持續維護與週期性審查

原文內容 Original Text

Following validation of the new microbiological method and accompanying system, appropriate procedures should be established to maintain the system in a validated state; for example, standard operating procedures should be implemented, the method and accompanying system should be included in a change control program, and all instrumentation or equipment should be maintained in good working condition.

Once the new method and accompanying system has completed validation, subsequent change controls should assess the impact of any change on the validation status of the system. Additionally, a formal mechanism should be put in place to periodically review the method and the accompanying system's performance, as well as the overall validation program in relation to current good practices.

A special area of concern is the preventive maintenance program, frequently handled by the supplier of specialized equipment. Many programs include updating system software with the most current software versions, extension of databases, and periodic calibration checks. It is important to ensure that appropriate requalification testing is performed before placing the system back into use, that the end user is cognizant of good practice requirements, and that they perform the maintenance accurately in addition to documenting the activities that were performed. The supplier should also provide information on the potential impact of repairs or spare-part changes, or drift in instrument performance, in order to define requalification or verification requirements.

Finally, the results or outputs of this phase should feed back into the first phase of the validation process, namely, the risk assessment. This will ensure that the overall operation of the new method and accompanying system does not introduce additional quality risks associated with the lifecycle of the products and sample matrices that will be tested routinely.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

Change Control (變更管制): 驗證完成後，任何軟體更新、資料庫擴充、耗材批次變更都必須先評估其對「已驗證狀態」的衝擊，才能執行，這是維持系統合規性最核心的機制。

Requalification (再確效): 設備經過維修、校正、軟體更新或零件更換後，重新執行部分或全部確效（IQ/OQ/PQ）以證明系統仍維持原有效能，通常由供應商說明潛在影響範圍後界定再確效需求。

Periodic Review (週期性審查): 定期（而非僅在出事後）檢視方法與系統的整體績效，並將結果回饋到最初的風險評估，形成一個閉環的生命週期管理循環。

比喻說明 Analogy

這就像一輛通過驗車的自駕巴士：驗證合格（PQ 通過）不代表可以永遠不管它。定期換機油、韌體更新前要先在測試場地試跑（再確效），每年還要做一次全面體檢（週期性審查），並把體檢結果回饋給最初設計巴士安全規範的工程團隊（風險評估），才能確保這台車終其服役生涯都維持在「可信賴」的狀態。

重點提示 Key Notes

供應商執行的預防性維護（例如軟體升級、資料庫擴充）常被誤認為「例行保養、不影響驗證狀態」，但原文明確要求：維護完成後，系統重新上線前必須執行「適當的再確效測試」。CDMO 在與供應商簽訂維護合約時，應要求供應商主動提供「維修/零件更換/效能飄移對驗證狀態的潛在影響」說明，作為決定再確效範圍的依據，而非被動等待稽核時才追溯。

實務應用 Practical Application

某 CDMO 導入的黴漿菌 NAT 系統廠商每半年會推送一次軟體更新，內含菌種資料庫擴充。若品保與驗證團隊未事先在變更管制程序中定義「資料庫更新是否觸發再確效」，一旦稽核員發現系統版本已變更但無任何再確效紀錄，將直接構成資料完整性（Data Integrity）缺失。建議在供應商合約與內部 SOP 中，把「軟體/資料庫版本變更觸發哪一層級的再確效（僅文件審查、部分 OQ、或完整 PQ）」明訂為變更管制矩陣的一部分，避免各廠站各自解讀。

練習思考 Practice Questions

  1. 為什麼週期性審查的結果需要「回饋到風險評估」，而不是只回饋到 SOP 修訂？
  2. 如果供應商的預防性維護包含更換偵測器（Detector）零件，你會如何界定再確效的範圍？
  3. 變更管制程序中，哪些類型的變更「一定」需要再確效？哪些可能僅需文件化即可？
5.4.15 Decommissioning of Equipment 設備除役

原文內容 Original Text

Decommissioning is part of the equipment's lifecycle, and it must follow the same change-control rules to ensure that the qualified and calibrated state is maintained until the final retirement. Decommissioning (除役): 設備生命週期的最後一個受管制階段，本身視為一項「變更」，必須依循變更管制程序執行，而非單純的資產報廢作業。

比喻說明 Analogy

5.5.1.1 Limit of Detection 偵測極限

原文內容 Original Text

For the assessment of the LoD, the lowest amount of target nucleic acid in a sample that can be detected, the positive cut-off point is determined. The positive cut-off point is the minimum number of target sequences per sample-volume tested that can be detected in 95 percent of the test runs. To determine the positive cut- off point, at least three (3) independent 10-fold dilution series of characterized and calibrated working strains or standards should be tested. This design, with a sufficient number of replicates at each dilution, gives a total number of 24 test results for each dilution which enables a statistical analysis of the results.

The specificity of a NAT, the ability to detect unambiguously the mycoplasma target nucleic acid, depends on the primer and probe sequences and the stringency of the NAT conditions. Hence, the specificity experiments should include a range of bacterial species other than mycoplasmas, that is, microorganisms with a close phylogenetic relationship to mycoplasmas, such as other Gram-positive bacteria like Clostridium, Lactobacillus, Streptococcus, or others, to evaluate the possibility for cross-detection of the non-mycoplasma bacteria.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

Positive Cut-off Point (陽性判定臨界值): 在 95% 的測試批次中都能穩定偵測到的最低目標序列數量／樣品體積，是 NAT 方法 LoD 的統計定義，不同於一般 CFU 法用「最低可培養出菌落」的概念。

設計要求: 至少 3 個獨立的十倍序列稀釋，每個稀釋濃度需有足夠重複數，總計每個稀釋濃度需達 24 次測試結果，才足以支撐統計分析（例如機率單位迴歸 Probit Regression 常用於此類 95% 偵測率估計）。

Specificity（特異性）與交叉反應: 因為 NAT 是靠引子（Primer）與探針（Probe）辨識特定核酸序列，必須用親緣關係接近黴漿菌的革蘭氏陽性菌（如 Clostridium、Lactobacillus、Streptococcus）挑戰，確認不會產生偽陽性交叉檢出。

比喻說明 Analogy

設定 Positive Cut-off Point 就像測試一支麥克風「最小能穩定收到聲音的音量」：你不會只測一次就下結論，而是要在多個安靜的房間（3 組獨立稀釋序列）、多次重複測試（每濃度 24 次）中，統計出「95% 的情況下都收得到聲音」的那個音量門檻，這樣才有統計上的信心。而特異性測試就像確認麥克風不會把隔壁房間類似頻率的雜音（親緣相近菌種）誤判成你要找的那個聲音來源。

重點提示 Key Notes

24 次測試結果（3 組稀釋序列 × 8 重複，或其他組合達到 24 次）是 Eur. Ph. 2.6.7 設計邏輯的具體化，目的是讓 95% 偵測率的估計具備統計功效（Statistical Power）。若企業自行縮減重複次數，必須提出風險評估與科學論證，否則所定義的 LoD 可能無法通過稽核檢視其統計合理性。

實務應用 Practical Application

CDMO 在導入黴漿菌 NAT 方法取代傳統培養法時，LoD 驗證的 24 次測試設計會直接影響驗證時程與成本（每次測試涉及 PCR 試劑、標準品、人力）。建議在驗證計畫階段就與 QC 實驗室排定足夠的儀器與人力產能，並考慮把 LoD 研究與 5.5.1.3 的 Comparability Study 共用稀釋序列與樣品製備流程，以節省重複製備標準品的成本與時間。

5.5.1.2 Robustness 穩健性

原文內容 Original Text

The robustness of the NAT, the capacity of the NAT to remain unaffected by small, but deliberate variations in the method parameters, is assessed for example, by using different lots of the PCR-reagents, using different thermal-cycler devices, or testing of samples on different days by different analysts, to examine the variation between test runs. In addition, robustness of the alternative NAT can be evaluated by collaborative studies.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

Robustness (穩健性): 方法對「刻意製造的小幅參數變動」保持不受影響的能力，與 Ruggedness（Intermediate Precision，針對自然發生的操作變異，如不同分析員、不同日期）概念相近但著重點略有不同——Robustness 更聚焦於「刻意改變的方法參數」本身是否穩健。

典型變動因子: 不同批號的 PCR 試劑、不同型號的熱循環儀（Thermal Cycler）、不同日期/不同分析員操作，這些都是評估 NAT 穩健性時常見的挑戰變因。

比喻說明 Analogy

Robustness 測試就像確認一份食譜「換了不同品牌的麵粉、換了不同烤箱、換了不同的廚師」做出來的蛋糕，味道跟品質是否依然一致。如果換個牌子的試劑或換一台熱循環儀就讓 NAT 結果差很多，代表這個方法對細節太敏感，日常大量生產環境下容易出錯。

重點提示 Key Notes

原文特別提到可透過「Collaborative Studies（協作研究/跨實驗室協同試驗）」評估穩健性，這代表單一實驗室內部的重複測試可能不足以完全支持穩健性宣稱，跨機構、跨儀器的協同驗證能提供更強的科學證據，尤其適用於供應商欲提交法規審查或建立業界共識標準的情境。

  2. 為什麼協作研究（跨實驗室）能提供比單一實驗室內部重複測試更強的穩健性證據？
5.5.1.3 Comparability/Equivalency Study 可比較性/等效性研究

原文內容 Original Text

To demonstrate comparability/equivalency between the culture methods, the indicator-cell culture method, and the NAT-based mycoplasma detection assay, cell-culture fluid should be spiked with relevant mycoplasma strain stocks. Based on this initially spiked sample (e.g., 100 CFU/mL), a serial tenfold dilution should be prepared using unspiked cell-culture fluid as diluent leading to samples spiked with 100 CFU/mL, 10 CFU/mL, 1 CFU/mL, 0.1 CFU/mL and, if needed, 0.01 CFU/mL. All dilutions are tested using all three different mycoplasma detection methods in parallel without freezing the samples.

Acceptance criteria for the comparability study:

  • The NAT must be able to detect at least 10 CFU/mL or less than 100 GC/mL if the NAT is planned as a replacement for the culture method.
  • The NAT must be able to detect at least 100 CFU/mL or less than 1000 GC/mL if the NAT is planned as a replacement for the indicator-cell culture method.
  • The NAT must be able to detect at least 10 CFU/mL or less than 100 GC/mL if the NAT is planned as a replacement for both the culture method and the indicator-cell culture method.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

三種比較對象: Culture Method（傳統培養法）、Indicator-Cell Culture Method（指示細胞培養法，用於偵測不易培養的黴漿菌株）、NAT-based Assay（核酸增幅檢測法）——comparability 研究要確認 NAT 至少不劣於它欲取代的那一種或兩種傳統方法。

序列稀釋設計: 以 100 CFU/mL 起始，做十倍序列稀釋至 0.1（甚至 0.01）CFU/mL，三種方法「平行」測試同一批未冷凍樣品，確保比較條件一致，排除凍融造成的活性差異干擾。

GC/mL (Genomic Copies per mL，每毫升基因體拷貝數): NAT 訊號的定量單位，與 CFU 是不同的計量系統——CFU 只計算「有活性且可培養」的菌，GC 則計算「核酸拷貝數」，兩者之間常存在 GC/CFU 比值差異（詳見 5.5.3）。

比喻說明 Analogy

這就像要證明一支新的體溫感應手環（NAT）能取代傳統的水銀溫度計（Culture Method）與電子耳溫槍（Indicator-Cell Culture Method）。你不能只測一次體溫，而是要讓三種工具同時、平行地測量同一群人在不同「發燒程度」（十倍序列稀釋）下的讀數，並且訂出手環至少要能偵測到多低程度的異常，才夠格說它可以取代前面兩種舊工具中的一種或兩種。

重點提示 Key Notes

三條可接受標準的邏輯差異值得注意：取代「培養法」或「同時取代兩者」都要求 NAT 至少偵測到 10 CFU/mL（<100 GC/mL）；但若只是取代「指示細胞培養法」，門檻放寬到 100 CFU/mL（<1000 GC/mL）。這反映了指示細胞培養法本身的靈敏度通常低於傳統培養法，因此 NAT 要取代它時，可接受的最低靈敏度要求也相應較寬鬆——驗證設計必須先明確「NAT 打算取代哪一種既有方法」，才能套用正確的一組標準。

實務應用 Practical Application

對細胞治療（Cell Therapy）或 ATMP 產品的 CDMO 而言，黴漿菌 NAT 檢測的最大商業價值在於「大幅縮短放行時間」——傳統培養法需要 28 天，NAT 可在數小時內完成。但若企業目標是同時取代培養法與指示細胞培養法（例如做為單一放行檢測），驗證設計就必須套用最嚴格的「10 CFU/mL / <100 GC/mL」標準，而非誤用較寬鬆的「取代指示細胞培養法」標準，否則會在法規申請或稽核時被要求重新驗證，嚴重延誤產品上市時程。

5.5.2 Test for Interfering Substances/Method Suitability Testing 干擾物質測試/方法適用性測試

原文內容 Original Text

For each new product or cell source, a test for interfering substances must be performed to demonstrate that the sample tested does not interfere with the NAT and/or the enrichment step (if applicable). Typically, samples from three different lots of the product to be assessed or batches of the cell-culture harvests (if available) are spiked with a maximum of 10 CFU/mL or 100 GC/mL and tested by the validated NAT to demonstrate sufficient recovery (e.g., 95%) of the target nucleic acid in the presence of the product to be tested.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

Test for Interfering Substances (干擾物質測試): 對應 TR 33 通則中的 Method Suitability（見 5.3.2），確認「特定產品/細胞來源」本身不會抑制或掩蔽 NAT 訊號，是每一個新產品/新細胞來源上市前都必須執行的產品專屬測試，不能用其他產品的驗證數據替代。

Enrichment Step (增菌步驟): 部分 NAT 流程在核酸萃取前會先做增菌培養，以提高低濃度黴漿菌的可偵測性；干擾物質測試也必須確認產品基質不影響此步驟。

驗證設計: 三個不同批次的產品/細胞收穫液，以最高 10 CFU/mL 或 100 GC/mL 的挑戰濃度接種，要求 NAT 仍能回收至少 95% 的目標核酸訊號。

比喻說明 Analogy

這就像每次換一種新口味的湯底，都要重新確認金屬探測器（NAT）能不能在湯裡找到藏著的小硬幣（黴漿菌核酸）——濃稠的奶油湯（高蛋白/高黏度產品基質）可能會遮蔽訊號，讓探測器失靈，因此不能只在清水（標準稀釋液）中測過一次就宣稱對所有湯底都適用，每一種新湯底都要重新測試。

重點提示 Key Notes

95% 回收率的門檻與 5.3.2 通則中「Quantitative methods should recover at least 50% of the challenge microorganisms」的一般要求相比明顯更嚴格，反映出黴漿菌 NAT 屬於高風險放行檢測（尤其對細胞與基因治療產品），對偽陰性的容忍度極低。此外，「每一個新產品或細胞來源」都必須個別測試，意味著多產品平台型 CDMO 在承接新案時，干擾物質測試會是產品專屬（product-specific）驗證負擔，須提早納入專案時程規劃。

實務應用 Practical Application

對承接多元細胞治療專案的 CDMO 而言，每接一個新客戶/新細胞株，就必須重新執行一次干擾物質測試（三批次、95% 回收率），這是黴漿菌 NAT 快速放行方法在「跨產品可攜性」上的天生限制，與 5.3.2 中「可用風險評估豁免相似基質重複測試」的彈性原則不完全相同——因為黴漿菌測試風險等級高，通常不建議援引「相似基質」豁免干擾物質測試。建議在客戶報價與專案排程階段，就把此項測試列為每個新案必要的固定成本與前置時間，避免驗證進度被低估。

5.5.3 Mycoplasma Stocks and Standards 黴漿菌菌種庫與標準品

原文內容 Original Text

For the primary method validation and the product-specific test for interfering substances, use of a broad range of mycoplasma, including challenging organisms (based on risk assessment), is recommended.

The selection process should consider the following:

  • Manufacturing process (such as use of mammalian, avian, or insect cell expression systems)
  • Use of mammalian or plant-derived media components
  • Product category (biopharmaceutical, ATMPs, or live vaccines) and the occurrence of contamination (based on literature or their own experience)

Ph. Eur. Chapter 2.6.7 provides a suggestion of mycoplasma strains for validation testing based on their frequency of occurrence as contaminants phylogenetic relationship.

The mycoplasma strains can be viable cultures, a preparation of nonviable whole organism derived from these cultures, nucleic acid standards (e.g., the WHO International Standard for mycoplasma DNA for nucleic acid amplification technique-based assays designed for generic mycoplasma detection), or assay control plasmids. Positive control cultures should not be more than 15 passages from isolation, enumerated (harvested during the exponential phase of growth to guarantee a high viability of the culture and titered in CFU prior to freezing), and characterized using both the supernatant and cellular fractions, for genomic copies (GC), that is, their GC/CFU ratio. A GC/CFU ratio of the reference preparations of less than 10 is normally accepted by the relevant authorities; higher values must be justified.

The selection of the mycoplasma standard or reference material should be appropriate to the detection technology employed and should reflect the complete analytical workflow, including any pre-treatment or pre-processing steps (e.g., filtration, enrichment, nucleic acid extraction, lysis, concentration, or inactivation procedures). For culture-based methods, viable organisms are generally required to evaluate growth characteristics and recovery. For NAT or other molecular-based assays, nucleic acid standards or appropriately characterized inactivated preparations may be suitable. When pre-processing steps are part of the validated method, the chosen standard should allow assessment of the entire method performance, not solely the detection step.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

菌株選擇的三大考量: 製程使用的細胞表現系統（哺乳類、禽類、昆蟲細胞）、培養基是否含哺乳類或植物來源成分、產品類別（生物製劑、ATMP、活病毒疫苗）與其歷史污染紀錄——這些決定了風險評估下應挑戰哪些黴漿菌菌株。

菌株形式: 可為活菌培養物、非活性全菌製劑、核酸標準品（如 WHO International Standard）或檢測用控制質體，須依偵測技術特性選擇適合的形式。

Passage 限制與 GC/CFU 比值: 陽性對照培養物須在分離後 15 代以內、於指數生長期採收以確保高活性，並同時定量上清液與細胞部分的基因體拷貝數（GC），計算 GC/CFU 比值；此比值 <10 通常被主管機關接受，超過需額外論證。

比喻說明 Analogy

GC/CFU 比值就像檢查一批「種子」的發芽率與完整性：如果每一顆活的種子（CFU）對應的 DNA 拷貝數（GC）過高，代表這批種子摻雜了大量已死亡但 DNA 仍完整的殘骸，用它來標定「檢測極限」會失真——因為 NAT 偵測到的是 DNA，若標準品裡「死的比活的多很多」，會讓 LOD 看起來比實際更靈敏，卻無法反映真實產品中活菌污染的偵測能力。這也是為什麼要求比值 <10 並限制培養代數（Passage）與採收時期。

重點提示 Key Notes

最後一段的原則極為關鍵：標準品的選擇必須反映「完整的分析流程」，包含前處理步驟（過濾、增菌、核酸萃取、裂解、濃縮或去活化）。若驗證方法中包含增菌或萃取步驟，卻選用「已萃取好的純核酸標準品」直接跳過這些前處理步驟做驗證，將無法真正評估整體方法效能，只驗證到偵測步驟本身——這是黴漿菌 NAT 驗證中常見的設計缺陷，稽核時容易被指出「標準品未能代表完整工作流程」。

實務應用 Practical Application

對於使用昆蟲細胞表現系統生產病毒載體的 CDMO，菌株選擇時應特別參考該表現系統歷史上曾發生的黴漿菌污染案例（例如某些節肢動物來源培養基成分風險），而非直接套用哺乳類細胞常見的黴漿菌菌株清單。同時，若驗證流程包含增菌步驟，應堅持採購「活菌培養物」而非「純化核酸標準品」作為驗證用標準品，即使活菌操作在生物安全管理上成本較高，也應優先滿足「反映完整分析流程」的法規期待，避免日後被要求重新驗證。

本節重點回顧 Section Summary

  • 生長型方法的最短培養時間可用「無條件進位」、「加碼一整天」或「世代時間換算公式 T = tTTR + log2(10)·G」三種方式設定安全係數，選擇時須權衡科學保守性與放行時效的商業考量。
  • non-CFU 訊號的 AMM/RMM 不應強行套用傳統 CFU 為基礎的警戒/行動水準，而應依 Decision-Equivalence 概念，透過基準值、百分位數法或 OOT 趨勢規則建立科學上可辯護的新可接受標準，並視需要與法規機關溝通。
  • 驗證完成後的持續維護（變更管制、預防性維護、再確效）與週期性審查須形成閉環，回饋至最初的風險評估；除役同樣須遵循變更管制邏輯，確保設備生命週期終點仍具完整文件追溯性。
  • 黴漿菌 NAT 方法驗證的三大支柱為 LOD（Positive Cut-off Point，需 3 組獨立十倍稀釋序列共 24 次測試）、Robustness（試劑批號、儀器、分析員/日期變異，可搭配協作研究）、Comparability/Equivalency（依欲取代的方法不同，分別套用 10 CFU/mL 或 100 CFU/mL 的最低偵測門檻）。
  • 每個新產品/細胞來源皆須執行干擾物質測試（三批次、95% 核酸回收率），且菌株與標準品的選擇須反映完整分析流程（含前處理步驟），並將 GC/CFU 比值控制在 <10 以確保標準品代表性。
