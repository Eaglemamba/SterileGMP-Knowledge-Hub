1.0 Introduction 導論

原文內容 Original Text

Technical Report No. 33 (Revised 2026): Evaluation, Validation, and Implementation of Alternative and Rapid Microbiological Methods (TR 33) is intended to provide guidance for the successful evaluation, validation, and implementation of alternative/rapid microbiological methods (AMM/RMMs) used in the pharmaceutical, biotechnology, advanced therapy medicinal products (ATMPs), medical devices, and related industries. The use of these methods is aligned with the expectations for improving product and process quality, contamination control, and the rapid release of medically necessary drugs and devices. Applications may include, but are not limited to, sterility testing, microbiological examination of nonsterile products (enumeration and test for specified microorganisms), antimicrobial effectiveness testing, microbiological monitoring of cleanrooms and other controlled environments, analysis of pharmaceutical grade water, microbial characterization and identification, and microbiological in-process control testing.

The intended audience of TR 33 includes microbiologists and related individuals who are responsible for the validation and utilization of microbiological test methods in the microbiology testing laboratory, manufacturing environment, or other relevant testing settings. This technical report may also be of interest to suppliers of testing equipment, microbiology managers and supervisors, validation specialists, quality control (QC), compounders, and quality assurance personnel responsible for approving product release and validation protocols, manufacturing personnel, individuals who work on global compendial initiatives, and regulatory agencies. Additionally, Section 9.0 (Appendix 1) through Section 11.0 (Appendix 3) provides useful information to support statistical analysis of data from AMM/RMM validation and comparability studies.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

AMM/RMM (替代與快速微生物學方法): 泛指所有能取代傳統平板培養、以更快速度或更高精準度偵測微生物的技術，可能是「自動化傳統方法」、「使用替代性偵測標記（surrogate markers）」，或是「全新技術平台」三種形式之一。

TR 33 的定位: 這份技術報告不是規範「該用哪一種 AMM/RMM 儀器」，而是提供一套「如何證明 AMM/RMM 與傳統方法一樣可靠（甚至更好）」的驗證邏輯與方法學。

比喻說明 Analogy

可以把 CMM（傳統微生物方法）想像成「沖洗底片」：你把樣品送進培養箱，要等 2～14 天讓菌落長出來，才知道結果——就像底片要送到暗房沖洗，隔天才能看到照片。而 AMM/RMM 就像「數位相機」：透過螢光訊號、ATP 生物冷光、阻抗變化等技術，幾小時甚至幾分鐘內就能看到結果，而且影像（數據）品質往往更清晰、更少受人為判讀誤差影響。

重點提示 Key Notes

本章特別點名 ATMP（細胞治療、基因治療等先進療法）為 TR 33 適用範圍之一。對於像 Amaran 這樣的臨床階段無菌充填 CDMO 而言，若客戶產品線涉及短效期、小批量的細胞/基因治療產品，AMM/RMM 的「快速放行」能力將直接影響病人能否及時用藥——這正呼應 TR 33 開宗明義提到的「rapid release of medically necessary drugs and devices」。

原文特別提到 Section 9.0（附錄 1）至 11.0（附錄 3）提供統計分析支援——這暗示 AMM/RMM 的驗證並非單純「跑一次實驗看結果」，而是需要嚴謹的統計比對設計，這也是後續章節的重點。

1.1 Purpose & Scope 目的與範圍

原文內容 Original Text

Microbiological testing plays an ever-increasing role in pharmaceutical and related industry laboratories. In response to this, a variety of alternative and rapid methodologies have emerged in recent years that automate conventional microbiological methods (CMM), make use of surrogate markers, or are based on wholly new technologies. These AMM/RMMs can offer significant improvements in terms of speed, accuracy, precision, and/or specificity, as compared with conventional microbiology test methodologies.

Most of the microbial testing performed today continues to rely on centuries-old CMMs that are based on the recovery and growth of microorganisms using solid or liquid microbiological growth media. This is true, in part, because these methods can be appropriate for their intended use and have a long history of application in both industrial and clinical settings. However, they are often limited by slow microbial growth rates, the unintended selectivity of microbiological cultures, and the inherent variability of microorganisms in their response to growth-based methods. Despite the limitations of CMMs, alternative, more rapid, and potentially superior microbiological methods have not been universally adopted within the pharmaceutical, biotechnology, ATMP, medical device, and related industries. This seemingly continues to be due in part to a lack of understanding regarding the steps needed to demonstrate their equivalence or comparability to CMMs that would be acceptable to a firm's internal quality expectations as well as regulatory agency reviewers and inspectors. The original PDA TR 33 was published in 2002, and its first revision was released in 2013. Based on the industry, compendial, and regulatory developments since the 2013 revision, including the need for the rapid release of personalized medicines, such as ATMPs, sterile-compounded pharmaceutical preparations, radiopharmaceuticals, and the rapid release of therapeutic drug products needed in public health emergencies, the task force members believe that this revision of TR 33 is timely and will provide additional guidance to assist with the evaluation, validation and implementation of AMM/RMMs. While this document focuses on the manufacture of drug products, the validation approaches and principles defined herein can be translated to other product categories (e.g., consumer goods) where these AMM/RMMs may be applied.

Considerable guidance can be found regarding the validation of chemical methods, for example, U.S. Pharmacopeia (USP) General Chapter <1225> Validation of Compendial Methods, International Conference on Harmonisation (ICH) Quality Guideline Q2(R2): Validation of Analytical Methods, and U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) Guidance for Industry: Analytical Procedures and Methods Validation for Drugs and Biologics (1-3). These publications provide very specific instruction regarding the demonstration of alternative analytical chemistry methods and their equivalence to conventional chemical methods.

Chapters introduced by the global compendia, including USP General Chapter <1223> Validation of Alternative Microbiological Methods, European Pharmacopoeia (Eur. Ph.) Chapter 5.1.6 Alternative Methods for Control of Microbiological Quality, and Japanese Pharmacopoeia (JP) General Information G4-6-170: Rapid Microbial Methods, provide guidance on the steps needed to validate an AMM/RMM (4-6). Due to the differences in recommended validation strategies within each guidance document and the potential variability in validation expectations across regulatory agencies, additional guidance that provides an understandable and holistic approach to the qualification and implementation of AMM/RMMs is desirable.

This technical report was developed as a collaborative effort among microbiologists and expert consultants who have practical experience validating and implementing AMM/RMMs within the pharmaceutical, biopharmaceutical, and medical device industries, AMM/RMM suppliers, and regulatory agency representatives. By providing updated and agreed-upon performance standards and industry best practices, the task force believes the validation and implementation of AMM/RMMs will be greatly accelerated.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

CMM (傳統微生物學方法): 以固體或液體培養基培養、回收微生物為基礎的檢驗方法，例如平板培養法（Plate Count）、薄膜過濾培養法等，是目前藥廠微生物實驗室的主流做法。

Equivalence / Comparability (等效性／可比對性): 這是 AMM/RMM 能否被接受的關鍵門檻——必須用科學數據證明新方法偵測微生物的能力「不劣於」甚至「優於」CMM，才能說服公司內部品質系統與各國監管機關。

比喻說明 Analogy

USP <1223>、Eur. Ph. 5.1.6、JP G4-6-170 三份指引各自對「如何驗證 AMM/RMM」有不同的具體要求，就像同一趟國際貨運要分別通過美國、歐盟、日本三個海關——每個海關的檢查清單、文件格式都不完全一樣。TR 33 的角色，就是提供一套「整合式的報關文件範本」，讓廠商準備一次完整的驗證資料包，就能同時因應多國法規審查的期待。

重點提示 Key Notes

TR 33 的修訂時間軸值得留意：2002 年首版 → 2013 年第一次修訂 → 2026 年本次修訂。這次改版的驅動力明確指向個人化醫療（ATMP、無菌調劑製劑、放射性藥品）與公衛緊急事件下的藥品快速放行兩大需求——這也解釋了為何近年 CDMO 產業對 RMM 導入的興趣快速上升：小批量、短效期產品若仍採用傳統 14 天無菌測試，往往等不到放行、病人已錯過治療窗口。

依照決策優先序（無菌保證 → 產品關鍵品質屬性 → 商業與彈性），導入 AMM/RMM 的核心價值主張永遠必須先站在「無菌保證不打折」的立場上，快速放行只是等到「等效性驗證」通過之後才能享有的附加效益，不能本末倒置。

實務應用 Practical Application

假設 Amaran 承接一項 ATMP 客戶的無菌充填委託案，其產品保存期限僅 72 小時，傳統 14 天無菌培養法完全無法配合放行時程。QA 部門評估導入某款自動化 RMM 系統以縮短放行週期。此時必須先完成的工作，正是 TR 33 所描述的等效性/可比對性驗證——同時準備符合 USP <1223>、Eur. Ph. 5.1.6（若產品外銷歐盟）的驗證文件包，並在驗證通過前，不得以「客戶急件」為由跳過或簡化驗證步驟。

本節重點回顧 Section Summary

  • TR 33 提供 AMM/RMM 評估、驗證、導入的整合性指引，適用於藥品、生技、ATMP、醫療器材等產業，應用場域涵蓋無菌測試、環境監測、藥用水質檢驗等
  • CMM 仍是主流方法，但受限於生長速度慢、選擇性偏差、微生物反應變異性等固有缺點
  • AMM/RMM 遲未普及的關鍵瓶頸，在於缺乏清楚的「等效性/可比對性」驗證路徑，而非技術本身不成熟
  • 本次 2026 年修訂的驅動力，來自 ATMP、個人化醫療、公衛緊急事件對快速放行的迫切需求
  • USP <1223>、Eur. Ph. 5.1.6、JP G4-6-170 各自提供驗證指引，但要求不完全一致，TR 33 旨在提供更整合、易懂的驗證架構
  • 無論商業壓力多大，無菌保證（Sterility Assurance）永遠是決策優先序的第一位，快速放行必須建立在等效性驗證通過的基礎上
