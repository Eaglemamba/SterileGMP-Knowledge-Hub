11.0 Appendix 3: Validation of Alternative and Rapid Microbiological Methods: Additional Statistical Analysis-Non-CFU Signals 附錄三：替代/快速微生物學方法驗證之額外統計分析——非 CFU 訊號

原文內容 Original Text

Several AMM/RMMs detect or enumerate microorganisms that are not based on the principle of growth, e.g., they may measure molecules such as nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide plus hydrogen (NADH) or riboflavin or other cellular targets that are indicative of viable cells. Thus, such AMM/RMMs can detect or quantify microorganisms regardless of whether they can be cultured on or in conventional growth media or if they are non-culturable, due to stress, injury, being in a dormant state and/or other physiological conditions. This results in a reported signal that is not a CFU, but rather, some other designation based on the scientific principle of the AMM/RMM. For example, biofluorescent particle counters (BFPCs) will report the recovery of microorganisms in terms of an autofluorescent unit. Additionally, some non-CFU methods operate continuously, generating significantly more data points than a single, growth-based result. Accordingly, it may be more complicated to compare the results from non-CFU AMM/RMMs with the results from CFU-based methods (18).

As just stated, a non-CFU method may report non-culturable signals in addition to what would be expected to be reported in a CFU-based method (i.e., culturable signals). Indeed, in its simplest statistical form, the total number of microorganisms XM in a sample can be conceptually defined as a sum of two components, i.e., XM = XCFU + XNCFU, with XCFU being the number of viable and culturable microorganisms present in the test sample and XNCFU the viable and non-culturable number of microorganisms in the test sample. The CMM, which relies on microorganism growth, would only respond to the XCFU component and does not signal anything from the XNCFU component. For quantitative methods it is expected that the CMM will produce a number that is at most equal to XCFU, because the CMM quantifies microorganisms in terms of CFU's and not in the actual number of microorganisms that may be present in the test sample. Alternatively, the AMM/RMM with a non-CFU signal would typically respond to both components XCFU and XNCFU in the sample.

Based on this statistical formulation of the non-CFU signal and assuming that the AMM/RMM and CMM perform equivalent to the XCFU component, it may be expected that the non-CFU AMM/RMM is more sensitive than the CMM in detecting and enumerating microorganisms. However, this assumption may not always be true, as this will depend on a number of factors, e.g., the scientific principle of the AMM/RMM and its resulting detection signal, and the physiological state of the microorganisms being detected. In this case, the non-CFU method may report similar recoveries as the CFU-based method. Thus, the AMM/RMM can be expected to perform at least identical to, or alternatively, superior to, the CMM, even if the AMM/RMM is performing slightly worse than the CMM solely on the XCFU component. Here, the additional signal of the AMM/RMM for the XNCFU component would add to the signal of the XCFU component. Therefore, demonstrating non-inferiority of the AMM/RMM to the CMM only on the total number of microorganisms XM in a validation study could imply that the AMM/RMM is inferior to the CMM on the XCFU component. On the other hand, if the validation experiments can generate test samples that only or mostly contains the XCFU component, the non-inferiority analyses discussed in Section 9.0 (Appendix 1) and Section 10.0 (Appendix 2) may be applicable, since a comparison between the AMM/RMM and CMM is then a direct comparison on the CFU signal. However, it may not be possible to construct such an experiment with 100% certainty since there will always be some level of non-culturable cells in a microbial challenge suspension, even under the best conditions. Therefore, using a non-inferiority statistical test when comparing a non-CFU-based method with a CFU-based method may not be appropriate, hence a statistical test based on a superiority model may be more appropriate.

An additional element to the issue of generating more presence tests or enumerations than the CMM which also plays a role in the validation of the AMM/RMM with a non-CFU unit, is the method's performance on blank samples (i.e., the test sample is free of viable microorganisms). Some AMM/RMMs may detect or quantify non-viable microorganisms or non-microbial particles (XNM) in addition to viable microorganism (XCFU and XNCFU) and respond to the total number of particles (XP = XNM + XM = XNM + XCFU + XNCFU) in the test sample. The AMM/RMM could then produce signals for test samples that are free of viable microorganisms resulting in a false positive rate for blank samples with qualitative microbiological methods or a false positive count or background signal for quantitative microbiological methods. When the AMM/RMM detects or quantifies non-viable microorganisms or non-microbial particles, a comparison with the CMM becomes more difficult since it is unclear how much of the signal for the non-viable microorganisms or non-microbial particles XNM is dominating the signal from the XCFU and XNCFU components. It is for this reason that an understanding of the false positive rate is well understood before performing comparative testing against the CMM, which is described in the method suitability section of this technical report. For example, non-CFU AMM/RMMs that detect non-viable cells or non-microbial particles may or may not be suitable for use, depending on the extent of the recovery and whether the false positives or background noise can be subtracted from the true viable recovery counts under routine use.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

Non-CFU 訊號: 傳統 CMM（Conventional Microbiological Method，傳統微生物學方法）仰賴微生物「生長繁殖」才能被偵測到——沒有長出可見菌落，就沒有訊號。但部分 AMM/RMM 是偵測 NADH、riboflavin 等「活細胞代謝標記」，即使該微生物受壓力、受損或處於休眠狀態而無法在培養基上生長（non-culturable），只要細胞仍存活（viable），這類方法依然能偵測到訊號。

XM = XCFU + XNCFU 的統計拆解: 這是本章（乃至整個 Section 11.0）最核心的統計架構。樣本中「總存活微生物數」被拆成兩塊：可培養（XCFU，CMM 看得到）與存活但不可培養（XNCFU，CMM 看不到、但 non-CFU AMM/RMM 看得到）。CMM 的訊號上限就是 XCFU；而 non-CFU AMM/RMM 理論上會同時回應 XCFU 與 XNCFU 兩者。

XP = XNM + XM（總粒子數）: 若儀器連非活性微生物或非微生物粒子（XNM，如死菌碎片、蛋白質聚集物、環境微粒）都會產生訊號，則總訊號變成 XP。這正是「偽陽性率」與「背景雜訊」的統計來源——訊號中混雜了「並非真正代表活菌」的成分。

比喻說明 Analogy

把 CMM 想像成「打卡系統」，只有员工實際刷卡進入辦公室（=生長出可見菌落）才會被記錄；而 non-CFU AMM/RMM 更像是「大樓門禁感應+體溫感測」系統，只要有活人（存活細胞）進出大樓，不論他有沒有去刷卡機打卡（不論可不可培養），都會被感應到。因此門禁系統（AMM/RMM）記錄到的人數，理論上會 ≥ 打卡系統（CMM）記錄到的人數——這正是 XM = XCFU + XNCFU 的直覺意義。如果門禁系統連「送貨機器人」（非活性粒子 XNM）都會誤觸發警報，那就是偽陽性/背景雜訊的來源。

重點提示 Key Notes

原文明確指出一項統計陷阱：若僅以「總微生物數 XM」證明 AMM/RMM 對 CMM 的非劣性（non-inferiority），可能掩蓋 AMM/RMM 在 XCFU 部分其實表現「劣於」CMM 的事實——因為 XNCFU 的額外訊號補足了整體分數。這對 CDMO 品保單位是關鍵警訊：驗證報告若只呈現總數比對通過，未必代表方法在「可培養菌」偵測能力上真的等效。

正因為「100% 純 XCFU 樣本」在實務上幾乎不可能建構（挑戰菌懸浮液中必然存在一定比例的不可培養細胞），原文建議：比較 non-CFU AMM/RMM 與 CFU-based CMM 時，應優先採用優越性檢定（superiority test）而非非劣性檢定（non-inferiority test）——這與 Section 9.0/10.0 針對「同為 CFU 訊號」方法所採用的非劣性架構形成鮮明對比，讀者應特別留意這個方法論上的轉折。

11.1 Qualitative Microbiological Methods with Non-CFU Signals 非 CFU 訊號的定性微生物學方法

原文內容 Original Text

This section will discuss two situations for non-CFU AMM/RMM methods, methods that have no or hardly any false positives, and methods that produce a relevant level of false positives. This distinction is made because false positives will improperly affect positive rates.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

兩種情境的分野: Section 11.1 依「偽陽性率是否顯著」把 non-CFU 定性方法分成兩類討論——11.1.1 討論幾乎沒有偽陽性的情況，11.1.2 討論偽陽性率具有實質影響的情況。原因很直接：偽陽性會混入 positive rate（陽性率）計算中，若不處理，會扭曲對「真正偵測能力」的估計。

比喻說明 Analogy

這就像評估一台新的煙霧偵測器：如果它幾乎不會因為做菜的油煙而誤報（無偽陽性），可以直接比較「真的失火時多快偵測到」；但若它常因油煙誤報（有偽陽性），就必須先扣除「誤報基準值」，才能公平比較兩款偵測器「真正偵測到火災」的能力。

重點提示 Key Notes

這個分類方式決定了後續統計方法的選用——11.1.1 可直接沿用 gMPN（generalized Most Probable Number）比值方法；11.1.2 則需要額外納入 blank samples（空白樣本）的資料，才能扣除偽陽性對 spiked samples（添加樣本）陽性率的「遮蔽效應（masking effect）」。規劃驗證方案時，應先確認方法的偽陽性率是否顯著，再決定採用哪一套統計架構。

11.1.1 Limit of Detection when the Non-CFU Alternative/Rapid Microbiological Methods Does Not (or Hardly) Detects False Positives 非 CFU 替代/快速微生物學方法幾乎不偵測到偽陽性時之偵測極限

原文內容 Original Text

In this example, a superiority analysis will be conducted using data presented in Table 11.1.1-1 to illustrate an experiment with a single organism. The positive rates (with exact 95% confidence intervals) are given by: 

AMM/RMM:  p_A = 0.933 [0.779; 0.992]
CMM:      p_C = 0.767 [0.577; 0.901]

The observed positive rate for the AMM/RMM is clearly larger than the positive rate for the CMM, which is expected because the non-CFU AMM/RMM responds to both the XCFU and XNCFU components and the CMM only responds to the XCFU component.

Testing the traditional null hypothesis H0: pA = pC can be done with Pearson's chi-square test or Fisher's exact test as discussed in Section 9.0 (Appendix 1). However, these two test statistics do not reject the null-hypothesis for the data in Table 11.1.1-1 at the significance level of 0.05, because the p-values are determined at 0.071 and 0.145, respectively (not shown here but can be obtained following the guidance in Section 9.0 (Appendix 1)). These statistics test the null-hypothesis against the alternative hypothesis H1: pA ≠ pC (e.g., the recovery of the AMM/RMM is statistically inferior or superior to the recovery of the CMM). The alternative hypothesis demonstrating that the AMM/RMM is statistically superior in detecting microorganisms (i.e., a greater microbial recovery rate) than the CMM: H1: pA > pC is more relevant for non-CFU AMM/RMM methods. This superiority hypothesis can be better evaluated by calculating the ratio Rpr = pA/pC of the positive rates and using an appropriate lower 95% confidence limit on this ratio, similar to the non-inferiority analysis in Section 9.0 (Appendix 1). If the Farrington and Manning confidence limit is used, a ratio of Rpr = 1.217 with a lower 95% confidence limit equal to 1.018 is obtained. This implies that the hypothesis of superiority has been demonstrated, i.e., H0: pA = pC is rejected in favor of H1: pA > pC superiority, since the value one (1) is below the lower 95% confidence limit.

For a non-inferiority analysis, as described earlier in Section 9.2.2, it was suggested to use the gMPN when the positive rates were high, because the ratio of positive rates would converge to one when the spiked concentration level increases, and the lower 95% confidence limit would easily become above a non-inferiority margin of, for example, 0.70, irrespective of how inferior the AMM/RMM is against the CMM. For superiority testing this is less of a concern, since superiority would require a lower 95% confidence limit above the value one (1) and this is not so easy to determine if the true positive rate of the AMM/RMM is equal to or less than the true positive rate of the CMM.

Nevertheless, the gMPN which can be used here as well and applied to the data in Table 11.1.1-1, estimates the ratio of detection proportions RgMPN = θA/θC equal to RgMPN = 1.861 with a lower 95% confidence limit of 1.064. Here the formula from Section 9.2.3 was used. Thus, the gMPN shows that the null-hypothesis of equal detection proportions H0: θA = θC is rejected in favor of superiority H1: θA > θC, indicating that the AMM/RMM detects test samples with a single microorganism better than the CMM. It is appropriate to note that the calculated ratio of 1.861 actually represents a ratio of (1 + q)(θA/θC), with q = λNCFU/λCFU, λCFU the mean number of viable and culturable microorganisms and λNCFU the mean number of viable and non-culturable microorganisms present in the test samples. Thus, the real ratio of detection proportions θA/θC, which would investigate the detection of viable and culturable microorganisms, may not be superior θA/θC < 1. However, if the samples in the experiment contain a ratio q = λNCFU/λCFU of non-culturable and culturable microorganisms that is natural for routine sampling, then the AMM/RMM detects better than the CMM, due to a performance on detecting non-culturable microorganisms.

Thus, both the approach on positive rates and the gMPN approach show that the AMM/RMM detects microorganisms better than the CMM.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

Superiority Analysis（優越性分析）: 與 Section 9.0/10.0 的非劣性檢定不同，本節示範用假設檢定 H1: pA > pC（或 θA > θC）來證明 AMM/RMM「確實優於」CMM，而非僅僅「不劣於」。判定標準是：比值（Rpr 或 RgMPN）的下限（lower 95% CI）要高於 1。

Rpr vs RgMPN 的差異: Rpr = pA/pC 直接比較觀察到的陽性率，較保守（本例下限僅 1.018，勉強超過 1）；RgMPN = θA/θC 則透過偵測比例（θ）模型估計，統計效率較高、信賴區間較穩健（本例下限 1.064）。兩者殊途同歸，皆支持優越性結論。

(1+q)(θA/θC) 的關鍵警示: 本節最重要的統計洞察在於：計算出的 RgMPN = 1.861 其實不是「純粹的偵測能力比值 θA/θC」，而是被 (1+q) 放大過的數字，q = λNCFU/λCFU。也就是說，AMM/RMM 表現優越，很可能是「贏在偵測不可培養菌」，而非「贏在偵測可培養菌本身」——後者（θA/θC）甚至可能 < 1。

比喻說明 Analogy

想像兩位保全人員巡邏同一棟大樓：保全 A（AMM/RMM）配備熱感應鏡頭，能發現「所有活著、有體溫的人」，包括躲在陰暗角落靜止不動的人（不可培養微生物）；保全 B（CMM）只能靠「聽到腳步聲、看到明顯走動的人」才算數（可培養微生物）。保全 A 的「發現人數」自然比保全 B 多，但這不代表保全 A 在「發現正在走動的人」這件事上，一定比保全 B 厲害——他很可能只是「贏在發現靜止的人」而已。這正是 (1+q)(θA/θC) 想提醒讀者的陷阱：總體優越 ≠ 核心能力（θA/θC）優越。

重點提示 Key Notes

本例中，傳統的 Pearson chi-square（p=0.071）與 Fisher's exact test（p=0.145）在 0.05 顯著水準下都無法拒絕虛無假設——也就是說用最保守、最傳統的雙尾檢定，看不出兩方法有顯著差異。但改用「單尾優越性檢定」（Rpr、RgMPN 的下限 CI）後，卻能證明優越性成立。這突顯了選對統計假設方向（雙尾 vs 單尾優越性）對驗證結論的決定性影響，稽核時應檢視驗證方案是否於實驗前就已預先指定正確的假設方向，避免事後選擇性套用檢定方法（p-hacking 疑慮）。

Clearly, this would not be an ideal situation, but it may still be desirable to know whether the AMM/RMM detects viable microorganisms as good as or better than the CMM. A direct comparison of the microbiological methods on spiked material alone may provide an incorrect conclusion, since the false positive rate masks or increase the true positive rate. In such situations, it may be helpful to combine data from test results of blank samples with data from spiked samples. To illustrate a statistical approach for such an experiment, it will be assumed that the following data has been collected (Table 11.1.2-1). The same data used in Table 11.1.1-1 was also used for Table 11.1.2-1 for the spiked samples. The statistical approach can be programmed in Excel from the formulae provided in Section 11.1.2.

A statistical model that may describe the positive rate pA for the AMM/RMM, and extends the gMPN approach, is described by pA = 1 − (1 − ηA)exp{−θAλ}, with θA the detection proportion, ηA the false positive rate, and λ the spiked concentration level (9). In case no microorganisms are present in the test samples, the positive rate pA become equal to ηA and when the false positive rate ηA = 0, the model reduces to the gMPN approach discussed in Section 9.2.3. For the CMM, a similar model could be assumed but with different parameters ηC and θC, where the false positive rate could be equal to zero (ηC = 0).

To understand whether the non-CFU AMM/RMM detects microorganisms better than the CMM testing the null-hypothesis H0: θA = θC against the superiority alternative hypothesis H1: θA > θC may be performed, irrespective of how the false positive rates ηA and ηC compare to each other. The ratio RM = θA/θC can now be estimated by: 

R_M = [ log(1 − p_A^0) − log(1 − p_C^0) ] / [ log(1 − p_A^+) − log(1 − p_C^+) ]

Where:

p_A^0  =  positive rate for blank samples with AMM/RMM
p_C^0  =  positive rate for blank samples with CMM
p_A^+  =  positive rate for spiked samples with AMM/RMM
p_C^+  =  positive rate for spiked samples with CMM

Calculating these positive rates with their exact 95% confidence intervals from the data in Table 11.1.2-1, results in the data seen in Table 11.1.2-2.

Using the positive rates, the ratio of detection proportions can be determined, using the formula for RM above. The ratio of detection proportions then becomes RM = 1.763. Note that this ratio is lower than the ratio RgMPN = 1.861 calculated with the gMPN method on the data of Table 11.1.2-1 in Section 11.1.1, because the false positive rates for the AMM/RMM is subtracted in some form from the positives obtained at the spiked ratio to compensate for the masking of false positives obtained at the spiked samples. Similar to the analysis of the data without false positives, the ratio represents (1 + q)(θA/θC), again with q = λNCFU/λCFU, λCFU the mean number of viable and culturable microorganisms and λNCFU the mean number of viable and non-culturable microorganisms present in the test samples.

A lower 95% confidence limit on the ratio RM can then be calculated by: 

LCL_M = R_M · exp{ −1.645 · sqrt( τ_A²/ξ_A² + τ_C²/ξ_C² ) }

Where:

ξ_A   =  log(1 − p_A^0) − log(1 − p_A^+)
ξ_C   =  log(1 − p_C^0) − log(1 − p_C^+)
τ_A²  =  p_A^0 / (n_A^0 (1 − p_A^0)) + p_A^+ / (n_A^+ (1 − p_A^+))
τ_C²  =  p_C^0 / (n_C^0 (1 − p_C^0)) + p_C^+ / (n_C^+ (1 − p_C^+))
n_A^0 =  number of test samples for the AMM/RMM at the blank samples
n_A^+ =  number of test samples for the AMM/RMM at the spiked samples
n_C^0 =  number of test samples for the CMM at the blank samples
n_C^+ =  number of test samples for the CMM at the spiked samples

The lower 95% confidence limit, using the data in Table 11.1.2-1, is then determined by LCLM = 0.989. Based on this lower 95% confidence limit, it can no longer be concluded that the non-CFU AMM/RMM detects microorganisms better than the CMM.

However, it could be concluded that the non-CFU AMM/RMM is non-inferior to the CMM when detecting microorganisms at a non-inferiority margin of 0.7 (or at even higher values), but this does not mean that the AMM/RMM is non-inferior on detecting culturable microorganisms (i.e., detecting the XCFU part of the test sample), since the test samples may have contained several XNCFU components and the AMM/RMM could be inferior on the CFU part of the test samples. This issue may also be the case when the lower 95% confidence limit is above the value one (1) (demonstrating superiority), but this may be less likely since the non-CFU AMM/RMM should be more sensitive than the CMM. Finally, showing non-inferiority with an estimate of the ratio of detection proportion above the value one (1) may still provide enough evidence that the non-CFU AMM/RMM is sensitive enough.

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

Masking Effect（遮蔽效應）: 若 AMM/RMM 對空白樣本也會產生偽陽性，直接拿 spiked samples（添加樣本）的陽性率去比較，會高估其真實偵測能力——因為偽陽性「墊高」了 spiked 樣本的陽性率。解法是同時蒐集 blank samples（空白樣本）與 spiked samples 的數據，用兩者共同回推真正的偵測比例 θ。

擴展的 gMPN 統計模型: pA = 1 − (1 − ηA)exp{−θAλ} 是本節的核心模型——它把「偽陽性率 ηA」與「偵測比例 θA」拆開來估計。當樣本中無微生物（λ=0）時，pA 自動退化為 ηA；當 ηA=0（無偽陽性）時，模型退化回 Section 9.2.3 的標準 gMPN 模型——這正是 11.1.1 與 11.1.2 兩套方法的數學橋樑。

RM 與 LCLM: RM 是扣除偽陽性影響後的偵測比例比值；LCLM 則是其下限信賴區間，透過 ξ（估計量）與 τ²（變異數）計算而得，判定邏輯與 RgMPN 相同：下限 > 1 → 優越；下限介於非劣性邊界（如 0.7）與 1 之間 → 僅能宣稱非劣性。

11.1.3 Discussion on Fit for Purpose 適用性目的之討論

原文內容 Original Text

Superiority in detecting microorganisms with the non-CFU AMM/RMM compared to the CMM may create challenges in making GMP decisions in daily practice when a positive result of the non-CFU AMM/RMM is being compared with the established CFU-based acceptance levels for microbial recoveries. Such a positive result may be indicated in four situations. First, the sample may only contain viable and culturable microorganisms (XCFU) and the non-CFU AMM/RMM would be expected to detect these. This outcome would most likely be confirmed by the CMM and the use of AMM/RMM or CMM would typically not result in a different decision (i.e., decision equivalence).

Second, a positive test in the AMM/RMM is due to the presence of only viable but non-culturable microorganisms (XNCFU). The CMM would most likely not confirm the presence of these microorganisms, indicating a possible difference in decision making (i.e., non-equivalent decision making). In this case, the non-CFU AMM/RMM would be correct in detecting these microorganisms, which would not be detected in the CMM as they would not be expected to produce a growth-based signal.

Third, a positive test in the AMM/RMM is due to the presence of both viable and culturable and viable but non-culturable microorganisms (XCFU + XNCFU). The CMM may not confirm the presence of the culturable organisms if the culturable signal XCFU is far below the LoD, also indicating a possible difference in decision making (i.e., non-equivalent decision making), since the AMM/RMM would be expected to detect when culturable and non-culturable organisms are beyond the LoD. Here, the non-CFU AMM/RMM would be correct in detecting all of these microorganisms, but would not be detected in the CMM due to a too small culturable signal.

The fourth situation is that the non-CFU AMM/RMM provided a false positive and no viable microorganisms are actually present, in which case the CMM would most likely not confirm the presence of microorganisms either (i.e., again non-equivalent decision making). However, as previously discussed, care must be taken if false positives are detected in the AMM/RMM, as decisions should only be made based on the presence of viable cells and not non-viable cells or non-microbial particles. Accordingly, superiority of the non-CFU AMM/RMM in this case may lead to non-equivalent and more importantly, inappropriate decisions on the presence of supposed microorganisms in the test sample.

How well the non-CFU AMM/RMM and CMM agree in their decision-making on the same set of samples is not so easy to determine, because the agreement between the AMM/RMM and CMM would depend on many factors (e.g., the number of microorganisms in the sample, the LoD of the microbiological methods, the false positive rates, the ratio of culturable and non-culturable microorganisms, and the dependency between XCFU and XNCFU). However, for specific settings and under certain conditions, the degree of agreement between the AMM/RMM and CMM could be determined. Note that agreement is defined here as the expected probability of obtaining the same decision on the set of test samples.

In the scenario that a test sample does not contain microorganisms, the agreement is equal to: PAGREE = pA0pC0 + (1 − pA0)(1 − pC0). For the data in Table 11.1.2-1, the agreement (expressed in percentages) is equal to 86.7%, indicating decision equivalence between the AMM/RMM and CMM for blank samples. Note that good or strong agreement levels are 80% or more for both inter-rater agreement and questionnaire reliability (19, 20).

For settings with samples containing only viable but non-culturable microorganisms, agreement between the AMM/RMM and CMM is determined by the probability: 

P_AGREE = π_A(X_NCFU) · p_C^0 + [1 − π_A(X_NCFU)] · [1 − p_C^0]

with πA(XNCFU) the probability of a presence result of the AMM/RMM for a sample containing XNCFU microorganisms. When the number XNCFU in a sample is large enough, the AMM/RMM is likely to detect this and the agreement PAGREE drops or converges to the false positive rate PAGREE = pC0 of the CMM, implying a very low agreement and emphasizing the arguments above that superiority induced by non-CFU AMM/RMM-detectable cellular targets (other than culturable microorganisms) causes disagreement in decisions on samples without culturable microorganisms.

Finally, for samples containing both viable and culturable, and viable but non-culturable microorganisms (XM = XCFU + XNCFU), the agreement is given by: 

P_AGREE = π_A(X_NCFU + X_CFU) · π_C(X_CFU) + [1 − π_A(X_NCFU + X_CFU)] · [1 − π_C(X_CFU)]

with πA(XCFU + XNCFU) the probability of a presence result of the AMM/RMM for a sample containing XCFU + XNCFU microorganisms and with πC(XCFU) the probability of a presence result of the CMM for a sample containing XCFU culturable microorganisms. Clearly, when the non-CFU AMM/RMM is superior to the CMM and the number of viable and culturable microorganisms is above the LoD of the CMM, the agreement would be at least PAGREE = 90%. This indicates that decision equivalence between the AMM/RMM and CMM is attained when the number of culturable microorganisms is above the LoD of the CMM. On the other hand, the agreement could also become substantially lower than 90%, particularly when πA(XNCFU + XCFU) ≈ 1 and πC(XCFU) ≈ 0 (the culturable signals are below the LoD); the agreement would then be close to zero again.

To obtain an agreement level for sample from routine practice, there is a need to balance the three different levels of agreement as just described above, since these situations could possibly occur in different frequencies. The expected agreement for the statistical models that were used for the data in Table 11.1.1-1 and Table 11.1.2-1 have been calculated, under the assumption that the two components XCFU and XNCFU are Poisson distributed and that they would enter the samples independently. The formula for agreement is then provided by: 

P_AGREE = 1 − (1 − p_A^0) exp{−λ_M · θ_A} − (1 − p_C^0) exp{−λ_CFU · θ_C}
            + 2(1 − p_A^0)(1 − p_C^0) exp{ −λ_M · θ_A − λ_CFU · (1 − θ_A) · θ_C }

Where:

θ_C     =  detection proportion of the CMM
θ_A     =  detection proportion of the AMM/RMM
p_C^0   =  false positive rates of the CMM
p_A^0   =  false positive rates of the AMM/RMM
λ_CFU   =  mean number of viable and culturable microorganisms
λ_M     =  mean number of viable microorganisms (given by the sum of culturable and
           non-culturable viable microorganisms, λ_CFU + λ_NCFU) present in the samples

For overdispersed numbers of microorganisms and dependent components XCFU and XNCFU, extension of the agreement probability is needed. Figure 11.1.3-1 shows a few examples of the level of agreement between the AMM/RMM and CMM as function of λCFU: i.e., the mean number of viable and culturable microorganisms in the samples.

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

四種陽性情境與 Decision Equivalence（決策等效性）: 原文將 AMM/RMM 呈陽性時的樣本組成拆成四種情境：①純 XCFU（CMM 通常也會確認 → 決策等效）、②純 XNCFU（CMM 通常不會確認 → 決策不等效，但 AMM/RMM 才是對的）、③XCFU+XNCFU且 XCFU 低於 LOD（CMM 不會確認 → 決策不等效，AMM/RMM 仍是對的）、④偽陽性、實際上無活菌（CMM 不會確認 → 決策不等效，且這次是 AMM/RMM 錯了）。前三種情境中 AMM/RMM 的判斷都是正確的，唯獨第四種情境的「不等效」才是真正需要警惕的偽陽性風險。

PAGREE（一致性機率）: 定義為「AMM/RMM 與 CMM 對同一組樣本做出相同放行/拒收決策」的期望機率。這是評估 non-CFU 方法是否 fit-for-purpose（適用於既定目的）的核心量化指標，而非僅看單一方法的靈敏度。

三種情境的一致性公式: 原文依樣本組成分別導出三條 PAGREE 公式——(a) 空白樣本（無菌）、(b) 純 XNCFU 樣本、(c) XCFU+XNCFU 混合樣本；並在假設 Poisson 分布且兩成分獨立進入樣本的前提下，整合成一條可依 λCFU 連續變化的一般化 PAGREE 公式，用於繪製 Figure 11.1.3-1。

比喻說明 Analogy

把 AMM/RMM 與 CMM 想像成兩位醫生，用不同儀器為同一批病人做診斷：醫生 A（AMM/RMM）用的新型影像儀器極為敏感，能發現連傳統聽診器（CMM）都聽不到的早期病灶（XNCFU）。當病灶明顯（重度污染）或完全沒有病灶（乾淨樣本）時，兩位醫生的診斷幾乎總是一致；但當病灶處於「剛好在傳統儀器偵測極限附近」的臨界地帶（0.5–3 CFU/sample），醫生 A 可能已經判定「陽性」，醫生 B 卻依然判定「陰性」——這正是兩人最容易產生「放行 vs 拒收」分歧決策的危險地帶，也是 Figure 11.1.3-1 U 形曲線谷底的意義。

重點提示 Key Notes

原文引用「80% 為 inter-rater agreement（評分者間一致性）與問卷信度的公認強一致性門檻」——本例空白樣本的 PAGREE=86.7%，符合此標準，代表兩方法在無菌樣本上的決策等效性良好。但這個 80% 門檻並非在所有污染程度下都能維持，Figure 11.1.3-1 顯示在 λCFU 介於 0.5–3 CFU/sample 的中間地帶，一致性可能大幅低於 80%（甚至低至 70% 左右）。

最重要的 GMP 決策啟示：non-CFU AMM/RMM 的「優越性」若源自偵測 XNCFU（第②③種情境），雖然科學上 AMM/RMM 是對的，但實務上會與既有的、以 CFU 為基礎建立的驗收標準（acceptance criteria）產生決策分歧——這代表導入 non-CFU 方法前，必須重新檢視放行標準是否需要調整，而非直接沿用 CFU-based 的歷史門檻，否則會頻繁出現「AMM/RMM 判陽性、但依 CMM 標準不會拒收」的爭議情境。

公式與計算 Formula

(a) 空白樣本一致性：

P_AGREE = p_A^0 · p_C^0 + (1 − p_A^0)(1 − p_C^0) = 86.7%   (Table 11.1.2-1 數據)

(b) 純 X_NCFU 樣本一致性：

P_AGREE = π_A(X_NCFU) · p_C^0 + [1 − π_A(X_NCFU)][1 − p_C^0]
        → 當 X_NCFU 夠大時，P_AGREE → p_C^0（一致性趨近 CMM 偽陽性率，代表嚴重不一致）

(c) X_CFU + X_NCFU 混合樣本一致性：

P_AGREE = π_A(X_NCFU+X_CFU) · π_C(X_CFU) + [1 − π_A(X_NCFU+X_CFU)][1 − π_C(X_CFU)]
        → X_CFU 高於 CMM 的 LOD 時，P_AGREE ≥ 90%；X_CFU 遠低於 LOD 時，P_AGREE → 0

(d) 假設 Poisson 分布且獨立進入樣本之一般化公式（用於繪製 Figure 11.1.3-1）：

P_AGREE = 1 − (1−p_A^0)exp{−λ_M·θ_A} − (1−p_C^0)exp{−λ_CFU·θ_C}
        + 2(1−p_A^0)(1−p_C^0)exp{−λ_M·θ_A − λ_CFU·(1−θ_A)·θ_C}

變數定義：

  • λCFU = 樣本中可培養活菌的平均數（X 軸變數）
  • λM = λCFU + λNCFU，樣本中存活菌總平均數
  • θA, θC = AMM/RMM、CMM 的偵測比例（與 LOD 直接相關）
  • pA0, pC0 = AMM/RMM、CMM 的偽陽性率

圖表解讀 Figure Analysis

Figure 11.1.3-1 以 λCFU（X 軸，可培養活菌平均數，對數尺度 0–10）對 Agreement %（Y 軸，65%–100%）繪出四條曲線。四條線分別代表不同的 θA/θC（偵測比例，與 LOD 相關）及 q=λNCFU/λCFU 組合：綠線（θ=0.8、q 較低）與藍線（θ=0.8、q 較高，λM=1.25λCFU）代表高偵測比例（LOD 較佳）的方法；紅線與黑線則代表 θC=0.5、θA=0.625（LOD 較差）的方法，其中黑線的 q 值又高於紅線。

四條曲線皆呈現「U 形」——在極低污染（<0.5 CFU/sample）與極高污染（>3 CFU/sample）時一致性都很高（>90%甚至接近 99%），但在中間地帶（λCFU≈0.5–2，谷底約在 1 附近）一致性會降到最低點：綠/藍線谷底約 77–83%，紅/黑線谷底更低至 70–73%。這代表 LOD 較差的方法（紅、黑線）與 q 值較高的方法（藍、黑線）在中間污染地帶的決策分歧風險更高。對實務操作而言，最重要的意涵是：污染程度落在方法 LOD 附近時，正是 AMM/RMM 與 CMM 最容易做出不同放行/拒收決策的高風險區間，應在方法導入評估與變更管理（change control）中特別著墨。

實務應用 Practical Application

假設 Amaran 正評估以 non-CFU AMM/RMM 取代環境監測（EM）現行的 CMM 平板法。若歷史數據顯示廠區潔淨室的典型汙染水準恰好落在 0.5–3 CFU/sample 這個「一致性低谷」區間，直接沿用 CMM 的歷史 OOS（out-of-specification）門檻可能導致 AMM/RMM 頻繁觸發「陽性」而 CMM 從未確認的爭議事件。建議做法：導入前先用 Figure 11.1.3-1 的方法論，針對廠區實際 q 值與兩方法的 θ 值繪製自身的一致性曲線，並與 QA、客戶事先溝通「決策不等效」的預期發生率與處理原則（如觸發調查而非直接判定 OOS），避免導入後才發現決策標準需要重新協商。

本節重點回顧 Section Summary

  • Non-CFU AMM/RMM 偵測的是「存活」而非「可培養」——總存活菌數可拆解為 XM = XCFU + XNCFU，理論上比 CMM 更敏感，但也因此不適合用非劣性檢定，應改用優越性檢定架構
  • Table 11.0-1 建立的符號系統（X、π(X)、p、θ、η、q、R、PAGREE 等）貫穿本節所有公式，是理解後續統計方法的基礎
  • 11.1.1 示範「幾乎無偽陽性」情境下的優越性檢定：Rpr、RgMPN 下限 CI 皆 > 1，但須留意 RgMPN 實際代表 (1+q)(θA/θC)，優越性可能只是贏在偵測不可培養菌
  • 11.1.2 示範「有相關偽陽性率」情境：須同時納入空白與添加樣本數據計算 RM，本例扣除偽陽性後 LCLM=0.989 < 1，優越性結論由「優越」翻轉為「僅非劣性」
  • 11.1.3 提出四種陽性情境與 decision equivalence（決策等效性）框架，並以 PAGREE 一致性機率量化——Figure 11.1.3-1 顯示中等污染程度（0.5–3 CFU/sample）是兩方法決策分歧風險最高的區間
  • 導入 non-CFU AMM/RMM 前，應評估其優越性是否伴隨決策等效性下降，並考慮是否需要重新協商以 CFU 為基礎的既有驗收標準
