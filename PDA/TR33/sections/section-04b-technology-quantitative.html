4.3 Quantitative Measurement Principles and Approaches 定量測定原理與方法

原文內容 Original Text

The primary objective of quantitative methods is to detect and quantify the presence of contaminating microorganisms, which can be used in risk assessments and may prohibit the use of the material or product.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

Quantitative Measurement (定量測定): 與 4.2 節的定性偵測(有/無)不同，定量方法回答的是「到底有多少？」——提供實際的菌數或濃度數字，可直接餵入風險評估模型，進而決定產品或原料是否可放行使用。

比喻說明 Analogy

這就像從家裡的「漏水警報器」(只告訴你有沒有漏水)，升級成「精密水表」(告訴你每小時漏了幾公升)。有了確切數字，你才能判斷問題的嚴重程度並採取對應行動。

重點提示 Key Notes

定量數據是建立警戒/行動限值(alert/action levels)與趨勢分析的基礎，直接支撐 Contamination Control Strategy (污染控制策略, CCS) 的風險決策——呼應決策階層中「無菌保證」與「產品關鍵品質屬性」的優先順序。

4.3.1 Imaging and Optics 影像與光學技術

原文內容 Original Text

Technologies using an ultra-high definition camera or three-dimensional (3-D) optical scanning can be used to detect and quantify microcolonies. High-resolution images taken at frequent intervals (automated kinetic counting) distinguishes growing colonies from debris by analyzing multiple images over time, and according to kinetic counting suppliers (i.e., personal communication), this could lead to a potential reduction of 50% less incubation time relative to human-based observation of colony growth. This approach allows for precise quantification and standardization of counts, facilitating early notifications of limit excursions, such as exceeding alert or action levels, as well as identifying out-of-specification results as soon as they are detected. Additionally, 3-D optical scanning enables high-resolution detection of colony formation and early identification of microcolonies.

Light scattering is a phenomenon in which the propagation of light is disturbed by its interaction with particles. Instrumentation that utilizes Mie scatter (i.e., where the scattered light intensity is dependent upon the particle size in a certain size range) and fluorescence detection can provide information about the size and number of viable microorganisms in an air or water sample, with the increased use of machine learning (ML) and algorithms used to interpret microbial presence.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

Automated Kinetic Counting (自動動態計數): 系統以固定時間間隔連續拍照，比對前後影像的「變化」來分辨正在生長的菌落與靜態的碎屑(debris)——會長大的才算菌落。Mie scatter (Mie 散射): 光線遇到特定粒徑範圍的粒子時，散射光強度會隨粒徑變化，藉此推算空氣或水樣中微生物的大小與數量。

比喻說明 Analogy

就像縮時攝影(time-lapse)記錄花朵開花的過程——重點不是單一張照片，而是「隨時間的變化」。會持續長大的是活菌落，靜止不變的只是灰塵或碎屑，這也是為什麼動態影像比單次終點判讀更準確、更快。

重點提示 Key Notes

供應商聲稱培養時間可縮短約 50%——這對 周轉時間 (Turnaround Time, TTR) 敏感的產品(如短保存期限的細胞治療產品)具有重大商業意義：更快放行代表更快交付病人，同時降低倉儲與延遲成本。

練習思考 Practice Questions

  1. 為什麼「多張影像隨時間比對」比「單一終點拍照」更能區分菌落與碎屑？
  2. 若導入自動動態計數技術能將培養時間縮短 50%，對你所屬 CDMO 的產能與交付排程會帶來哪些改變？
4.3.2 Fluorescent Probe Application 螢光探針應用

原文內容 Original Text

Viability-based technologies use metabolizable stains in combination with laser excitation for the detection and quantification of microorganisms, without the need for cellular growth. Viability-staining and laser-excitation can be used to detect and quantify single metabolizing cells and microcolonies. Using this approach, organisms that are stressed, injured, fastidious, or considered VBNCs may now be detected when these same organisms will not grow in or on conventional microbiological media. These types of technologies can be used for a variety of applications (e.g., microscopy, solid-phase and flow cytometry) that require the detection and enumeration of microorganisms, such as bioburden and microbial-limits testing, EM, process-water analysis, and sterility testing.

While providing broader detection capabilities, the accuracy of these technologies is dependent on signal-to-noise ratios and the specificity of the stain for the target metabolic activity. These platforms are applicable to microscopy, solid-phase cytometry, and flow cytometry for tasks such as bioburden testing, environmental monitoring, process-water analysis, and sterility testing, provided they are validated against compendial methods for equivalent recovery.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

Viable But Non-Culturable (存活但不可培養, VBNC): 指微生物實際上仍存活、具代謝活性，卻因受壓力、受損或本身生長要求嚴苛(fastidious)而無法在傳統培養基上長出菌落。螢光探針利用可代謝染劑(metabolizable stain)偵測「代謝活性」本身，而非依賴「能否長成菌落」，因此能捕捉到傳統平板計數會漏掉的活菌。

比喻說明 Analogy

就像用智慧手環偵測一個人的心跳，而不是要求他先跑完一整場馬拉松才能證明自己還活著。VBNC 菌就是「心跳仍在，但跑不動馬拉松(長不出菌落)」的狀態——螢光探針量的是心跳，不是馬拉松完賽證明。

重點提示 Key Notes

VBNC 對無菌保證意義重大：滅菌、防腐劑或環境壓力可能使微生物「受傷」而暫時失去可培養性，但仍具潛在致病力與代謝活性。若僅依賴傳統培養法，可能低估真實汙染風險——這正是決策階層中「無菌保證恆為第一優先」的具體體現。

  2. 訊噪比(signal-to-noise ratio)不佳時，可能導致哪一類判讀錯誤(偽陽性或偽陰性)？對放行決策有何影響？
4.3.3 Autofluorescence 自體螢光

原文內容 Original Text

Cellular autofluorescence is a property of all viable cells due to the presence of ubiquitous fluorescent biomolecules like NADH and riboflavin. Microbes emit autofluorescence when such endogenous fluorophores are excited by certain wavelengths of light. For example, microbes can emit autofluorescence in the yellow-green spectral region when illuminated with ultraviolet (UV) blue light due to the excitation of biomolecules including flavins, riboflavins and flavoproteins. An AMM/RMM example where autofluorescence is used is a digital imaging system that uses autofluorescence to enumerate micro-colonies. In such a system, test samples may be filtered, and the membrane is placed onto an agar surface and incubated. During this time, a light-emitting diode (LED) excites microcolonies to emit autofluorescence, which are then quantitated using a charge-coupled device (CCD) imaging system. Incubation can continue to allow for the recovery of larger colonies for subsequent analysis, such as microbial identification. Additionally, metabolizable fluorochromes can enhance signals from metabolically active colonies, improving method sensitivity.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

Autofluorescence (自體螢光): 所有活細胞內都存在 NADH、核黃素(riboflavin)等內生螢光分子，受特定波長光線激發後會自然發出螢光——不需額外添加任何染劑，即為「免染色(label-free)」的偵測方式。

比喻說明 Analogy

就像每個人身上都有獨特的體味，不需要噴香水就能被聞出來。自體螢光是微生物與生俱來的「天然signature」，相較於 4.3.2 節需外加染劑的螢光探針技術，這是完全不需試劑的偵測方式。

重點提示 Key Notes

此技術巧妙保留「過濾+瓊脂培養」的傳統流程，僅將人眼判讀替換為 LED 激發、CCD 影像量化，因此培養後仍可讓菌落持續長大，供後續鑑定分析使用——兼顧快速偵測與傳統確認鑑定的銜接，降低導入新技術的驗證風險。

練習思考 Practice Questions

  1. 自體螢光技術與 4.3.2 節的螢光探針技術，在試劑使用與偽陽性風險上有何本質差異？
  2. 為何保留「持續培養」的步驟，對後續微生物鑑定與根本原因調查(root cause investigation)特別重要？
4.3.4 Flow Cytometry 流式細胞術

原文內容 Original Text

Flow cytometry quantifies microbes as they pass the light source and detector in a defined flow path. In-line execution can excite existing cell chemistry (e.g., amino acids, nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide plus hydrogen (NADH), and riboflavins) often referred to as biofluorescent particle counters (BFPCs) or use a microbe-specific fluorophore for direct staining or metabolic activation (67). The detection of the emitted fluorescence provides confirmation of the presence and enumeration of a biological particle or microbe in the flow. Given known flow rates, the number of fluorescent particles per volume can be estimated.

Fluorescence excitation is enabled using a laser or LED of a specific wavelength with detection of the emitted light measured via a wavelength-specific detector (e.g., photo multiplier tube or CCD). For in-line flow systems, the distance between the light source and detector is limited by the optics used and the transparency of the carrier fluid. With cell-based chemistry, the discrimination of microbes relative to other interferant biological materials present in the sample makes validation and a direct correlation to cell count challenging for intended use (e.g., process monitoring or trending).

The use of a fluorescent probe, whether it is a direct stain or a metabolized stain, often limits the volume of the test samples (in mL), and preincubation of the sample with the stain prior to measurement is necessary. Additional sample-processing, such as filtration, may also be required to remove interfering fluorescent substrates. The incubation of the sample with targeted stain, whether a direct (live/dead) stain or the use of a nonfluorescent precursor that is enzymatically cleaved by a viability-indicator enzyme (e.g., esterase present in an intact metabolizing cell) to fluorescently label the cell, improves the differentiation of microbes versus other interferant materials. This differentiation can be further enhanced by equipment designed with narrow channels, such as microfluidics, which reduce the light-path length and allow for the testing of a wider variety of materials, including powders and opaque fluids.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

Flow Cytometry (流式細胞術, FCM): 讓樣品以定義流路(flow path)依序通過光源與偵測器，逐一量測每個粒子的螢光訊號，藉此確認並計數生物粒子。Biofluorescent Particle Counters (生物螢光粒子計數器, BFPC): 利用細胞本身既有的化學物質(如 NADH、核黃素)發出螢光，不需額外染色即可線上(in-line)偵測。

比喻說明 Analogy

就像機場的單一通道安檢閘門：每位旅客(粒子)一次只能通過一個掃描儀，系統逐一記錄每個人的特徵，而不是讓整群旅客一起量體溫。這種「逐一通過、逐一量測」正是流式細胞術的核心原理。

重點提示 Key Notes

已知流速(flow rate)即可換算出「每體積螢光粒子數」，但微生物與樣品中其他干擾性生物物質(如細胞碎片)難以完全區分，使得與傳統 CFU 計數的直接關聯性、驗證方式面臨挑戰——因此本技術目前較適合製程監測與趨勢分析用途，而非直接取代放行檢驗。

  2. 微流體(microfluidics)如何透過縮短光路長度，讓不透明流體或粉末也能被檢測？
4.3.5 Solid-Phase Cytometry 固相細胞術

原文內容 Original Text

Solid-phase cytometry relies on capturing microorganisms on a solid phase (e.g., a membrane filter) and incubating the sample with either a direct (e.g., DNA-specific dyes) fluorophore, labelled antibodies, or metabolite-sensitive fluorophores to indicate microbial presence. Microorganisms retained on a membrane are exposed to an excitation light source (e.g., LED or laser) at a defined wavelength, inducing fluorescence from viable cells or labeled particles. The emitted fluorescence is subsequently detected through an imaging or scanning system, such as a CCD-based camera or a photomultiplier tube, or analyzed by artificial intelligence (AI), enabling enumeration of viable microorganisms and/or total particles through image analysis.

As with flow cytometry, extended contact and incubation times can be used to maximize the signal from these fluorophores, track metabolic activity, enhance cellular recovery for slow-metabolizing or injured cells, and further differentiation relative to nonfluorescent, inert particles present in the sample. Sample considerations include the removal of interferant chemistries (e.g., fluorescent chemistry, excessive particulates), which may obscure viable particles in the image, and the impact of fluid/carrier characteristics on the technique (e.g., the filterability of the sample) (49, 68).

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

Solid-Phase Cytometry (固相細胞術, SPC): 先將微生物「捕捉」在濾膜等固相載體上濃縮，再進行染色與螢光激發偵測，最後以影像分析或 AI 進行計數——結合了「過濾濃縮大體積樣品」與「螢光快速偵測」兩者優勢。

比喻說明 Analogy

就像先用篩網把整條河裡的魚都撈到岸上濃縮，再一條一條檢查是死是活，而不是站在河裡邊游邊看(如流式細胞術的連續流動偵測)。先「固定濃縮」，再「逐一檢視」。

重點提示 Key Notes

與流式細胞術受限於樣品體積不同，固相細胞術可處理大體積、低生物負荷(low-bioburden)且可過濾的樣品——這使其自然銜接既有的薄膜過濾法(membrane filtration)無菌試驗流程，相對降低了驗證與法規銜接的負擔。

4.3.6 Hybrid Quantitative Identification Platforms 混合式定量鑑定平台

原文內容 Original Text

Hybrid technologies now exist that combine spectroscopic and spatial signatures for the detection of microorganisms using transformer-based multimodal spectral imaging. This platform enables label-free, non-growth-based analysis of individual cells to the species level, providing both quantification and identification simultaneously.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

Transformer-Based Multimodal Spectral Imaging (基於 Transformer 架構的多模態光譜影像): 結合光譜訊號(化學組成)與空間訊號(位置/形態)兩種資料，運用深度學習(transformer)模型同步分析，達到「不需生長、不需染色」即可同時完成計數與鑑定至物種層級。

比喻說明 Analogy

就像監視系統同時具備「計數鏡頭」(有幾個人)與「人臉辨識軟體」(這些人是誰)於同一台裝置中——一次掃描，同時取得數量與身份兩種資訊，不必分兩套系統各自運作。

重點提示 Key Notes

此技術代表 AI 驅動多模態分析的前沿趨勢，也是連接本節「定量測定(4.3)」與下一節「鑑定技術(4.4)」的橋樑——顯示未來 AMM/RMM 的發展方向是功能整合，而非單一技術各自為政。

4.4 Identification Techniques, Principles, and Approaches 鑑定技術之原理與方法

原文內容 Original Text

Microbial identification is determining the identity of a pure culture to genus, and preferably to species or subspecies, if required, using phenotypic or genotypic methods. As all identification methods have limitations due to their underlying technology and the defined scope and level of verification of the databases using other methods, and as there is no compendial microbial identification method, there is no reference method for comparative purposes. Hence, the validation frameworks described in USP <1113> and Ph. Eur. 5.1.6 are appropriate for microbial identification validation. Performing a verification or fitness-for-use approach as described in USP <1113> and Clinical Laboratory Standard Institute (CLSI) M52 Verification of Commercial Microbial Identification and Antimicrobial Susceptibility Testing Systems is recommended (69).

This verification would involve identifying a number of laboratory cultures appropriate for intended use of the instrument, followed by parallel identification of a consecutive number of in-house isolates using the CMM and new AMM/RMM identification method. Any discrepant results would be resolved by an independent testing laboratory and justified, based on the limitation of the technology and database, and possible changes in microbial taxonomy.

Once identified, an organism can guide risk management regarding material disposition.

For most AMM/RMM approaches, identification often requires an additional laboratory processing step once contamination using qualitative or quantitative techniques has been confirmed. Many identification techniques require further activation, enrichment, and purification of the source contamination for accurate identification.

The discrimination of the identification technique requires evaluation against a range of organisms related and relevant to your process (70). Conventional biochemical identification techniques can still provide a quick means of providing insight into the type of organism; however, the expansion from biochemical, metabolism-based analysis to colorimetric, analytical-based (spectral analysis) and molecular approaches has increased the speed and accuracy with which the specific identification of a microorganism can be made. Beyond the techniques summarized in Sections 4.4.1-4.4.5, the application of well-developed analytical techniques (e.g., Raman, FT-IR, GC-MS) and proteomic-based techniques (e.g., MALDI-TOF MS) used in microbial identification are only limited by their ease of application, the simplicity of their execution, and their relation to established databases similar to how DNA/RNA-based identification techniques have developed.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

Verification / Fitness-for-Use Approach (驗證/適用性確認法): 由於微生物鑑定並無單一標準「參考方法(reference method)」可供比對，USP <1113> 與 CLSI M52 建議改以驗證新方法是否「適合預定用途」為導向，而非傳統方法驗證中常見的「與金標準比對」邏輯。

比喻說明 Analogy

就像法院沒有一台絕對準確的「標準測謊機」可供比對，只能靠多方交叉檢驗(平行測試已知菌株與院內分離株)來建立對新方法的信心，而非直接對照一個公認完美的儀器。

重點提示 Key Notes

不一致的鑑定結果須交由獨立實驗室複核，並依技術/資料庫限制或微生物分類學(taxonomy)的可能變動加以說明——這是偏差調查(deviation investigation)文件中常被忽略卻至關重要的一環，直接影響鑑定結果能否支持風險管理決策(材料處置)。

  2. 當新舊鑑定方法結果不一致時，你的實驗室應建立哪些程序來解決與記錄這類差異？
4.4.1 Hyperspectral Imaging 高光譜影像

原文內容 Original Text

Hyperspectral scanning is an imaging technique that can be used as a presumptive identification approach and can be combined with the enumeration of colonies/microcolonies immobilized on a surface (e.g., agar plate/filter). This imaging technique uses a hyperspectral camera to detect color variations in the colonies across wavelengths that can cover the visible and near infrared regions (400-1,000 nm).

This technique relies on comparison to a reference, such as an internal database, to confirm genus- and species-level identifications. However, it may face limitations due to the availability and number of captured wavelengths, especially where these wavelengths are fixed.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

Hyperspectral Imaging (高光譜影像): 捕捉菌落在可見光到近紅外線(400-1,000 nm)範圍內、遠超出人眼可辨識的色彩變化細節，再與內部資料庫比對，做出屬(genus)、種(species)層級的「推定性鑑定(presumptive identification)」。

比喻說明 Analogy

一般相機只能捕捉紅、綠、藍三種顏色訊號，高光譜相機則像擁有數百個「顏色感測器」，能看見人眼看不出的微小色差，藉此分辨外觀長得非常相似的菌落。

重點提示 Key Notes

此技術屬「推定性(presumptive)」而非「確認性(confirmatory)」鑑定，且受限於相機可擷取的固定波長數量——實務上通常作為快速篩檢工具，仍須搭配其他確認鑑定技術(如 4.4.2-4.4.5 節)使用。

  2. 固定波長數量的限制，會如何影響高光譜影像技術對近緣物種的鑑別能力？
4.4.2 Matrix-Assisted Laser Desorption/Ionization Time-of-Flight Mass Spectrometry 基質輔助雷射脫附游離飛行時間質譜

原文內容 Original Text

Mass spectrometry systems, such as the MALDI-TOF MS, are used for microbial identification. Detection of specific microorganisms is an option, too, but the main application of MALDI-TOF MS is the identification of microorganisms. MALDI-TOF MS is an analytical technique in which analytes are separated and detected based on their mass-to-charge (m/z) ratio. Sample preparation typically involves one of three methods:

  • Direct Transfer Method: A small amount of a pure culture is applied directly to a target plate, followed by coating with a matrix solution (usually cinnamic acid). This method is recommended for bacterial identification.
  • Extended Direct Transfer Method: Formic acid is added to the sample on the target plate prior to coating with the matrix. This optimized procedure is recommended for the identification of bacterial isolates.
  • Formic-Acid Extraction Method: The isolate to be analyzed is purified in several steps by the addition of ethanol, formic acid, and acetonitrile before being applied to the target and coated with matrix. This procedure is recommended for the analysis of molds and yeasts.

After sample preparations, the procedure for each method is the same; during the crystallization of the matrix, the microorganism is embedded into the crystal structure. In the MALDI-TOF MS instrument, the sample spot is hit with a pulsed beam from a solid-state laser under a high vacuum. This vaporizes the matrix explosively and entrains the components of the microorganism (i.e., proteins) to establish the mass to charge ratio of the components.

The high energy of the laser favors the formation of ions, which are then accelerated in an electric field due to their charge. Subsequently, in a voltage-free field (i.e., flight tube), the separation of the analyte components takes place depending on their mass. The mass-to-charge ratio of individual particles of the microorganism determines the time of flight, that is, the time required for the particle to reach the detector after evaporation. Characteristic mass spectra result from the determination of the time of flight. Thus, for most bacteria, yeasts, or fungi—considering the fingerprint range between 4,000-12,000 Da—partly species-specific spectra can be distinguished. An automated comparison of the spectra generated by MALDI-TOF MS with a reference database is then carried out using software designed for this purpose.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

MALDI-TOF MS: 將菌體包埋於基質晶體中，以雷射脈衝將基質瞬間氣化並帶動蛋白質離子化，離子在無電場的飛行管(flight tube)中依質量分離，質量越小飛得越快——偵測器記錄「飛行時間」換算出質荷比(m/z)，形成該菌種獨特的「蛋白質質譜指紋」。

公式與計算 Formula

時間關係：飛行時間(TOF) 與 質荷比(m/z) 成正相關
　→ 質量越小/電荷越多 → 飛行時間越短 → 越早抵達偵測器

指紋辨識範圍：4,000 - 12,000 Da
　→ 落於此區間的蛋白質質譜圖譜，可用於部分物種層級的區分

比喻說明 Analogy

如同每個人獨一無二的指紋紋路，MALDI-TOF 將微生物的蛋白質質譜當作「分子指紋」，拿去資料庫比對，找出這個菌落究竟是誰。

重點提示 Key Notes

三種樣品前處理方法(直接轉移法、延伸直接轉移法、甲酸萃取法)分別對應細菌與黴菌/酵母菌等不同微生物類型，選錯前處理方法可能導致鑑定失準或失敗——此為 SOP 撰寫與操作員訓練的關鍵管控點。

練習思考 Practice Questions

  1. 為什麼黴菌與酵母菌需要採用甲酸萃取法，而不能直接使用細菌適用的直接轉移法？
  2. 若實驗室誤用不合適的樣品前處理方法，可能對鑑定結果的準確性造成何種偏差？該如何在 SOP 中預防？
4.4.3 Fourier-Transform Infrared Spectroscopy 傅立葉轉換紅外線光譜

原文內容 Original Text

Fourier-transform infrared spectroscopy (FT-IR) is an analytical technique used to capture high-resolution infrared spectra emitted or absorbed from a microbial cell. The sample is irradiated with infrared radiation, and the resulting spectrum is a plot of the intensity of the transmitted or reflected light as a function of wavelength. Comparing the microbial spectral profile to a database of known organisms allows for the identification and characterization of the organism being examined and is applicable to bacteria, fungi, and viruses.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

FT-IR: 以紅外線照射微生物細胞，依細胞內分子鍵結構的不同，產生獨特的吸收/穿透光譜(molecular fingerprint)，再與已知菌種資料庫比對進行鑑定與特性描述，適用範圍涵蓋細菌、真菌與病毒。

比喻說明 Analogy

就像用「敲擊回音」判斷房間的形狀與材質——紅外線照射細胞後，依分子鍵結構產生獨特的吸收模式，如同回聲頻譜洩露了房間(細胞化學組成)的資訊。

重點提示 Key Notes

相較於 MALDI-TOF MS 主要適用於細菌與真菌的蛋白質分析，FT-IR 的適用範圍更廣，涵蓋病毒(viruses)——但同樣高度依賴資料庫的完整性與品質，資料庫涵蓋不足的菌種將無法被準確鑑定。

  2. 若你的製程常見的汙染菌種不在資料庫中，FT-IR 鑑定結果應如何解讀與後續處理？
4.4.4 Raman Spectroscopy 拉曼光譜

原文內容 Original Text

Raman spectroscopy is an established analytical method based on the Raman-scattering properties of a material under evaluation. When laser light interacts with the sample, molecules are excited, resulting in a photon-energy shift related to molecular vibrations and rotations. The collection of the Raman-scattered light results in a unique spectral fingerprint, which is characteristic of the composition and molecular structure of a microorganism when immobilized on a surface (e.g., a filter). Comparative software and curated reference databases facilitate accurate identification in less than 15 minutes.

Because this is a chemistry-based technique, additional considerations are required for sample analysis, including selection of the appropriate immobilization background (e.g., Raman-negative filter/solid phase) and the subsequent isolation and removal of interferant chemistry from the targeted organism. This is important as these sample analysis considerations may impact the quality of the Raman spectrum.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

Raman-Scattering (拉曼散射): 雷射光與樣品分子交互作用時，因分子振動與轉動引發光子能量偏移，收集這些散射光即形成該微生物組成與分子結構特有的「光譜指紋」，配合軟體與資料庫可在 15 分鐘內完成鑑定。

比喻說明 Analogy

就像敲擊不同材質的杯子聽聲音判斷是玻璃、陶瓷還是金屬——雷射「敲擊」細胞後產生特定的「回音」(散射光子能量偏移)，不同分子結構的回音頻率各不相同。

重點提示 Key Notes

因屬化學層面的技術，固定化背景須選用「拉曼陰性(Raman-negative)」濾材，並排除干擾性化學物質——這與第 4.1 節「樣品效應考量(Sample Effect Considerations)」中背景干擾的風險完全呼應，凸顯樣品前處理在所有光譜/化學類技術中的共通重要性。

練習思考 Practice Questions

  1. 為什麼固定化背景材質必須是「拉曼陰性」？若使用不當材質會發生什麼問題？
  2. 15 分鐘的鑑定周轉時間，對即時放行或偏差調查的決策速度有何實務效益？
4.4.5 Nucleic Acid Amplification Techniques 核酸擴增技術

原文內容 Original Text

NAT employ a variety of scientific principles including, but not limited to, DNA-based PCR (e.g., touch-down PCR, real-time PCR, digital drop, and digital PCR), RNA-based reverse-transcriptase amplification, 16S rRNA typing, gene sequencing, and other novel techniques.

Many of these methods will detect the presence of a specific microorganism, such as an "objectionable" or pharmacopoeia-specified organism, or can provide a microbial identification, in some instances, to the strain or subspecies level. Additional methods can be used to estimate the number of viable microorganisms in a sample based on the number of amplification cycles or copy numbers required to reach a baseline or threshold level. Because there are many methods and systems based on NAT, it is not possible to cover all the scientific principles in TR 33; therefore, a brief example of key methods is provided, and additional information may be found in the literature and in online resources (36, 71-74).

It is important to distinguish microbial taxonomy from routine identification used in pharmaceutical microbiology laboratories. While taxonomy focuses on phylogenetic classification based on conserved gene regions (e.g., 16S rRNA or ITS), such sequence lengths may be insufficient to resolve closely related species. As a result, higher-resolution technologies, including whole-genome sequencing, have increasingly been adopted where greater discriminatory power is required (55).

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

Nucleic Acid Amplification Techniques (核酸擴增技術, NAT): 泛指所有以放大 DNA/RNA 訊號為原理的技術家族，包含 PCR 各類變體、反轉錄擴增、16S rRNA 分型與基因定序等，可用於偵測特定菌種，亦可用擴增循環數/複製數估算活菌數量。

比喻說明 Analogy

就像把一小段太模糊看不清的文字，重複影印上百萬份直到肉眼可讀——NAT 就是分子生物學的「影印機」，把原本量太少、無法偵測的 DNA/RNA 片段複製放大到可檢測的程度。

重點提示 Key Notes

務必區分「微生物分類學(taxonomy)」與「藥廠常規鑑定」的不同目的：分類學著重親緣分類，常用保守基因區域(如 16S rRNA、ITS)，但這些序列長度有時不足以區分近緣物種；此時需採用全基因體定序(whole-genome sequencing)等更高解析度技術。切勿將兩者的驗證要求與適用範圍混為一談。

  2. 你的實驗室在何種情境下應考慮採用全基因體定序，而非傳統 16S rRNA 定序？
4.4.5.1 Polymerase Chain Reaction 聚合酶連鎖反應

原文內容 Original Text

In a conventional PCR reaction, DNA is extracted from microorganisms (e.g., from an isolated colony on an agar plate) and heated to separate the double strands. DNA primers are then added that will bind to unique target sequences on the template DNA. The primer is elongated when a heat-stable DNA polymerase and nucleotide bases are added. The result is two new copies of the template DNA. This PCR process is then repeated, resulting in millions of copies of the target DNA. After the amplification step, the amplified nucleic acid targets must be analyzed using several techniques: amplicon detection/analysis in gel electrophoresis, detection by hybridization techniques (e.g., fluorescent-in-situ-hybridization or microarray-based techniques), or DNA-sequencing.

Real-time PCR uses unspecific nucleic-acid intercalating fluorescence dyes or target-specific probes labelled to detect the amplified nucleic acid after each amplification cycle but also allows a quantification of the target at timepoint t0. There are many different types of probes that bind to double-stranded DNA or to specific sequences as they amplify and accumulate in the test system. The increase in fluorescent signals is then detected. As an example, if a sample contains a DNA target sequence associated with a particular microorganism, following PCR amplification of that target, the fluorescence signal from the probe will be detected, and the system will provide a positive response for that microorganism. If the target is not present in the original sample, then no amplification will occur, and no fluorescence will be detected (above the threshold or background level). If the method uses real-time quantitative PCR, the DNA amplification reaction is measured as it occurs. Typically, fully automated real-time PCR systems are used, which reduces the hands-on time.

Digital PCR systems separate the sample to be tested into thousands of partitions using either nano well plates (with 20,000 partitions of 45 µL, 28,000 partitions of 30 µL, or even 100,000 partitions of 15 µL) or into droplets (digital droplet PCR). In these little partitions, the amplification using fluorescent probes takes place. After the amplification step, the nano well plate partitions or the droplets will individually be assessed for fluorescence. Partitions or droplets containing amplicons are detected by fluorescence and scored as positive. Partitions or droplets without amplicons are scored negative. A Poisson statistical analysis of the number of positive and negative partitions or droplets allows a quantitation of the target sequence.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

三代 PCR 技術演進：(1) 傳統 PCR——擴增後才以電泳/雜交/定序分析終點結果；(2) 即時 PCR (Real-time PCR)——每次擴增循環都偵測螢光訊號，可同步定量起始濃度(t0)；(3) 數位 PCR (Digital PCR)——將樣品分散至數千至數萬個微小分區，逐一判讀陽性/陰性，再以 Poisson 統計換算絕對濃度，不需標準曲線。

公式與計算 Formula

數位 PCR 分區規模範例：
  20,000 個分區 x 45 μL
  28,000 個分區 x 30 μL
  100,000 個分區 x 15 μL

原理：計數陽性/陰性分區比例
  → 套用 Poisson 分布統計模型
  → 反推樣品中目標序列的絕對濃度(不需標準曲線)

比喻說明 Analogy

數位 PCR 就像樂透包牌：把樣品分散進上萬個小格子(partitions)，每格「中獎」與否(有無擴增訊號)一翻兩瞪眼，再用統計方式(Poisson 分布)反推原始濃度——不需要事先建立對照的標準曲線。

重點提示 Key Notes

數位 PCR 免除標準曲線帶來的系統誤差，特別適合低生物負荷(low-bioburden)或黴漿菌(Mycoplasma)等需要絕對定量的應用情境——對細胞治療產品的原料/製程管控具有高度實務價值。

  1. 即時 PCR 與數位 PCR 在「定量」能力上的本質差異是什麼？
  2. 為什麼數位 PCR 不需要標準曲線也能達到絕對定量？這對驗證工作有何簡化效益？
4.4.5.2 Reverse Transcriptase Polymerase Chain Reaction 反轉錄聚合酶連鎖反應

原文內容 Original Text

Reverse transcriptase PCR (RT-PCR) uses RNA, instead of DNA, as a starting template for the PCR reaction. In this process, RNA is extracted from the cell, and the enzyme reverse transcriptase will create a complementary strand of copy DNA (cDNA). Ribonuclease H (RNase H) treatment will then remove the original single strand of RNA. A second primer and DNA polymerase is then used to create double-stranded cDNA, which will be used in the conventional PCR reaction previously described in Section 4.4.5.1. RT-PCR has some advantages over conventional PCR, such as a lower risk of contamination from nonviable cell DNA or residual DNA from the sample or work environment. RT-PCR is currently being used for the detection of specific types of microorganisms, such as viable, proliferating microorganisms, as well as the estimation of viable cell count.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

RT-PCR: 以 RNA(而非 DNA)作為起始模板，經反轉錄酶合成互補 DNA (cDNA)，再進行傳統 PCR 流程。由於 RNA 半衰期短、細胞死亡後迅速降解，偵測到 RNA 訊號更能代表「活菌」而非殘留死菌 DNA。

比喻說明 Analogy

就像檢查工地是否還有工人施工，看的是「還在冒的煙」(RNA，只有活細胞才持續產生，死後迅速消失)，而不是「已經蓋好的磚牆」(DNA，即使工人已經離開仍然存在)。

重點提示 Key Notes

相較於傳統 PCR 容易受「非活性細胞殘留 DNA」干擾產生偽陽性，RT-PCR 針對 RNA 的特性大幅降低此風險，特別適合活菌計數估算與環境監測(EM)——這是評估分子方法能否反映「真實活菌污染」的關鍵考量點。

練習思考 Practice Questions

  1. 為什麼以 RNA 為標的，能降低來自非活性細胞殘留 DNA 造成的偽陽性風險？
  2. 在環境監測情境中，RT-PCR 相較傳統 PCR 有何實務優勢？
4.4.5.3 Gene-Sequencing 基因定序

原文內容 Original Text

Gene-sequencing is used for the identification of a wide variety of microorganisms, including bacteria, yeast, mold, and Mycoplasma. The scientific principle involves sequencing each nucleotide base of a specific DNA target after PCR amplification. Typically, the first 500 base pairs (bp) of the 16S rRNA gene are used for the identification of bacteria, although the entire, approximately 1,500 bp long 16S rRNA gene has also been employed for greater accuracy. Sometimes, the 23S rDNA gene is sequenced to identify bacteria. Fungi, yeast, and molds are identified using the D2 region of a large subunit ribosomal RNA gene sequence. DNA is first extracted from a pure culture of cells and then amplified via PCR in four separate reactions—one reaction for each of the four deoxynucleotide bases: adenine (A), thymine (T), guanine (G), and cytosine (C). However, a mixture of these standard nucleotides and dideoxyribonucleotides (ddNTP) are used, where the latter nucleotides lack a 3'-hydroxyl (-OH) group on their deoxyribose sugar.

When a ddNTP is randomly incorporated during the amplification reaction, elongation of the PCR primer is terminated. This provides DNA fragments of varying lengths. Because each ddNTP is labelled with a different fluorescent dye, a series of fluorescently labelled copies of the amplified sequence, each terminating at a different base, is formed. These copies differ in molecular weight and can be separated and detected (based on their fluorescence) during gel electrophoresis of the reaction mixes. By simultaneously analyzing each of the four reaction mixes, representing the four deoxynucleotide bases, software within the gene-sequencer will reconstruct the linear arrangement of these bases in the sequence being analyzed. The resulting sequence is then compared with a library of known microorganism sequences and, if a sequence match is found, a genus and species identification are provided.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

Sanger 定序原理: 在標準核苷酸中混入無法延伸的雙脫氧核苷酸(ddNTP)，一旦隨機併入即終止該股延伸，產生長短不一、末端帶有不同螢光標記色的 DNA 片段。將四組反應以電泳分離、依螢光顏色重建序列，即可還原完整鹼基排列，再比對資料庫得出屬種鑑定。

比喻說明 Analogy

就像把一本書的每一頁，用不同顏色的書籤在不同位置卡住(終止延伸)，然後把所有書籤按長度與顏色依序排出來，就能還原出整本書的內容——這正是 ddNTP 鏈終止法還原 DNA 序列的原理。

重點提示 Key Notes

細菌鑑定常用 16S rRNA 基因「前 500 bp」(速度快)或「完整約 1,500 bp」(精度高)——這是速度與準確度之間的取捨，須在方法確效(method validation)文件中明確載明所選範圍及其理由。

練習思考 Practice Questions

  1. 使用 500 bp 部分序列與完整 1,500 bp 序列進行細菌鑑定，各自的優缺點是什麼？
  2. 為什麼黴菌、酵母菌需改用 D2 區域而非 16S rRNA 進行鑑定？
4.4.5.4 Next-Generation Sequencing 次世代定序

原文內容 Original Text

Next-generation-sequencing (NGS), also known as whole-genome-sequencing or high-throughput-sequencing, are novel technologies in nucleic acid analysis. In contrast to previously known sequencing methods, several hundred million fragments can be sequenced simultaneously in a sample and compared against a known library. NGS involves integration of fluorescently tagged deoxyribonucleotide triphosphates (dNTPs) into a DNA template strand during cycles of DNA synthesis. Fluorophore excitation at the addition of each nucleotide is used to identify the nucleotides during each procedure.

While NGS offers increased resolution and broad detection capability, interpretation of results from complex or mixed microbial populations may be challenging. Because NGS-based approaches sequence all nucleic acids present in the sample including background flora, non-viable organisms, and host-derived DNA, careful consideration must be given to data analysis strategies, relevance of detected organisms, and alignment with the intended application.

導師解析 Tutorial Commentary

核心概念解析 Key Concepts

Next-Generation Sequencing (次世代定序, NGS): 相較 4.4.5.3 節逐一分析的 Sanger 定序，NGS 可在單一樣品中同時定序數億條片段，並與資料庫大規模比對，大幅提升解析度與偵測廣度(全基因體層級)。

比喻說明 Analogy

就像同時用上百萬台印表機平行列印一本書的所有頁面，而不是像 Sanger 定序那樣一頁一頁排隊列印——速度快了非常多，但要把海量紙頁正確拼回一本完整的書(資料分析與判讀)，成了全新的挑戰。

重點提示 Key Notes

NGS 會將樣品中「所有」核酸都定序出來，包括背景菌相、非存活微生物與宿主來源 DNA——切勿未經資料分析策略篩選就直接將原始定序結果解讀為「存活污染」，這是導入 NGS 作為汙染調查工具時最容易誤判的風險點，須於數據判讀 SOP 中明確規範相關性篩選標準。

練習思考 Practice Questions

  1. 為什麼 NGS 偵測到的訊號不能直接等同於「存活微生物污染」？應如何篩選出有意義的結果？
  2. 相較於 Sanger 定序，NGS 的高通量特性對複雜/混合菌相樣品的判讀帶來哪些新挑戰？

本節重點回顧 Section Summary

  • 定量測定技術(4.3)——影像光學、螢光探針、自體螢光、流式與固相細胞術——共同解決「有多少活菌」的問題，各技術在樣品體積、干擾物排除與驗證難度上各有取捨。
  • 混合平台(4.3.6)將定量與鑑定整合於單一 AI 驅動系統，是 AMM/RMM 技術發展的前沿趨勢。
  • 微生物鑑定(4.4)因缺乏單一標準參考方法，須採用 USP <1113>、CLSI M52 的「驗證/適用性確認」框架，而非傳統的參考方法比對邏輯。
  • 鑑定技術可分為三大家族：光譜類(高光譜影像、FT-IR、拉曼光譜)、質譜類(MALDI-TOF MS)與核酸類(PCR、RT-PCR、基因定序、NGS)，各自在速度、解析度、適用微生物範圍與資料庫依賴程度上有所不同。
  • 選用任何 AMM/RMM 定量或鑑定技術時，仍須回歸決策階層：無菌保證優先，其次為產品關鍵品質屬性，最後才是商業與操作彈性的考量。
