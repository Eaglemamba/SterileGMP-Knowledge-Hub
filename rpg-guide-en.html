1Quick start

This is a game layer on top of the knowledge hub. The content is exactly the same documents you normally read; it just adds XP, stages and bosses, so you always know what to read next.

  1. Turn on adventure mode: press "Start adventure mode" on the dashboard, or add ?rpg=1 to any page URL, for example map.html?rpg=1. The same browser remembers it from then on. To turn it off, press "Turn off adventure mode" on the character page or add ?rpg=0.
  2. Create a character: pick a class, which is your department. Your class sets your required reading list and your route on the map.
  3. Follow "Next quest": the dashboard and the map point to the next document to read. Each section you finish earns XP.
  4. Take the section challenges: after each section, the page has a minion fight for it. Answer 80% correctly to pass.
  5. Defeat the zone boss and rank up: once every required document in a zone is cleared, challenge the boss. Win and you go up one rank.
Progress is stored in your own browser (no login needed). Before you switch computers or clear browsing data, press "Download save" on the character page to get a .json file. On the new computer, press "Load save" to bring it back; your adventure progress and reading history are restored.

Level and rank are different

  • Level (Lv): rises as XP adds up. It shows how much you have read.
  • Rank: Trainee → Foundation → Core → Advanced. It shows how far you are through your class's required reading. Each rank needs every required document in that zone cleared, plus a win against that zone's boss.
  • Level milestones: reaching certain levels gives a one-time reward that never affects scores. The character page shows the next milestone under the XP bar.

3Battle rules

Every battle is turn-based multiple choice:

  • Right answer: the enemy loses 1 HP.
  • Wrong answer: you lose 1 heart.
  • Hearts = the number of wrong answers the 80% pass mark allows + 1. For example, a 10-question fight allows 2 wrong, so you get 3 hearts.
  • Win or lose, every question shows its explanation and source (down to the subsection), so you can jump straight back to the original text.

Status effects

Statuses appear in section challenges and in boss, elite and Gatekeeper fights. They only affect hearts and shields and never change the score; the pass mark is still 80%. Mistake practice and Daily Patrol have no statuses.

Foe poisoned2 right answers in a rowPoison ticks once per turn; every 2 ticks restore 1 of your hearts. If you answer wrong, the foe uses the opening to cure itself.
Foe paralyzed3 right answers in a rowUntil the fight ends, each wrong answer has a 25% chance that the foe cannot move, so you lose no heart.
Foe asleepFighting a boss with a counter skill (Weak-Point Break)While it sleeps, wrong answers cost no heart; each turn it has a 1/3 chance to wake.

When you are poisoned, the poison only takes your "spare" hearts: while you can still reach 80%, poison will never knock you out or take your last heart.

Strengths and weaknesses

Each department skill has one status specialty, and every boss and elite is immune to, resists, or is especially weak to certain statuses. Before your first fight, a foe's resistances are unknown; after one fight they are recorded in the Intel Codex on the character page, and the skill menu before each fight shows the "Intel". Switch skills to try different strategies. Section challenges and Gatekeeper fights use your main skill.

Against ordinary foes, every specialty helps about the same; the difference is the foe's resistances. QA and Warehouse are defensive and ignore resistances; the other three do nothing against an immune foe. Example: a poison specialty is useless against the Particle Fiend, which is immune to poison. Switch to the Engineering paralysis specialty instead: combined with its weakness to paralysis, more than half of your wrong answers cost no heart.

Losing still pays

  • New-question bonus: in boss, elite and Gatekeeper fights, every question you answer right for the first time gives +3 XP, whether you win or lose. The same question never pays again, so farming one boss gives no XP; you need to see questions you have not met.
  • Finish bonus: answer all 10 questions of a boss or elite fight (without running out of hearts) and you get 1 material, win or lose, once per foe per day.
  • Gatekeeper: your first win gives +40 XP and 2 materials, and the document gets a Gatekeeper star on the map (up to 4 stars). Why wait a day? A test right after reading measures short-term memory; recalling a day later shows what you really kept.
Items, gear and skills only add hearts, shields or hints; they never change scoring. Passing always means 80% correct. Extra hearts will not pass you, but they help you last the whole fight, see more new questions and earn the finish material.

6Workshop: materials, gear, items

Craft things from materials in the Workshop on the character page.

Where materials come from

  • Passing a section challenge for the first time drops a material for that topic (10 kinds, such as Aseptic Crystal, Membrane Shard and Codex Page).
  • Right answers in a Review Ambush, and Mistake Book questions answered right "the next day" or later, also drop materials.
  • Your first win against a zone boss drops a Boss Crystal, used for high-tier gear and items.
  • Short on something? Trade 3 of one material for 1 of another (Boss Crystals cannot be traded).

7Other systems

Companions

After your first section, an egg hatches and you pick one of three companions. Each time you rank up, your companion evolves (4 stages in all), and it chats with you while you read.

Droplet Sprite→ Cloudsea Dragon
Owlet→ Cleanroom Sky Owl

Mistake Book

Every question you miss in any fight is recorded. It is only cleansed when you answer it right on a later day (re-answering right after reading the explanation does not count), and each cleansed question drops a material.

Review Ambush

The first time each day you open the dashboard, map or character page, a question may ambush you: due Mistake Book questions first, otherwise a section you passed 7 days ago. A right answer gives +15 XP and a material. It never interrupts you while you are reading a document.

Daily Patrol

An ambush is one random question; a patrol is a set you choose to spend three minutes on. "Daily Patrol" appears under the quest card on the dashboard and on the character page: up to 5 questions a day, drawn from sections you have passed and from your Mistake Book. Each right answer gives +15 XP; wrong answers cost nothing and go into the Mistake Book. Finishing it counts toward your learning streak for the day, drops 1 material, and stops that day's ambush. On days you read nothing new, a patrol still keeps your streak alive.

Titles and streaks

Topic titles level up as you clear documents on a topic. There are also hidden titles, such as a 7-day learning streak, passing three documents in one day, or getting every section right on the first try 5 times. The status bar at the bottom of the screen shows your streak.

Music and sound

The opening story has background music, with a new melody on each page; level-ups, rank-ups, new cards, boss entrances and battle results play short sound effects. Everything is synthesized live in the browser, so there are no audio files to download. To mute it, press "♪ Music on" in the corner of the opening or battle screen; your choice is remembered. Sound never affects any score.

8FAQ

Are the questions the same when I retake a challenge?

The questions are the same, but the option order of most questions is reshuffled every time, so you have to understand the content rather than remember "question 3 is C". Questions whose explanation names a letter (such as "option B") keep their original order so the explanation still matches.

What if I fail a section challenge?

There is no penalty. Read the explanations, go back to the source, and try again any time. Passing after failing twice or more earns the hidden title "Unyielding".

Why hasn't the boss appeared yet?

Every required document in the zone must be fully cleared (every section challenge passed) before the boss appears. The map or character page shows which ones are left.

Why is an elite boss's "Fight" button grayed out?

That elite has an entry requirement. Craft an item of the matching type at the Workshop and pick it before the fight.

Why do some documents have no questions?

Question banks are still being added. A document without one counts as cleared once you finish reading it, and its zone can be ranked up by reading alone.

Does this affect official training records?

No. The game only records progress in your browser, and the pass mark is 80%, the same as the Quiz Tracker.
