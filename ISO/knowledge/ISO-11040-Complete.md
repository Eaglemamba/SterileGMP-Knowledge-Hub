# ISO 11040 Prefilled Syringes Series (Parts 1-8): General and Specific Requirements for Prefilled Syringes

## Part 1: Parts 1-3: Dental Cartridge Components (Parts 1-3)

# ISO 11040 Parts 1–3: Dental Local Anaesthetic Cartridge Components

    

    

ISO 11040 Prefilled Syringes Series | Parts 1–3

    

        

### 1 Scope

        

This part of ISO 11040 specifies the design, dimensions, materials, performance and test methods for glass cylinders for dental local anaesthetic cartridges with a volume of 1.7 ml, 1.8 ml and 2.2 ml intended for single use only.

        

It applies to primary packs used in direct contact with a drug. The potency, purity, stability and safety of a drug during its manufacture and storage can be strongly affected by the nature and performance of the primary pack.

        

### 3 Dimensions and Designation

        

3.1 Dimensions: Glass cylinders shall have dimensions per Figure 1 including a truncated cone at the neck. The neck shall have:

        

            
- Height: (5 ± 0.2) mm

            
- Neck opening: (3.15 ± 0.15) mm over a depth of 5 mm

            
- Total length: (62.4 ± 0.4) mm for 1.7/1.8 ml; (75.0 ± 0.4) mm for 2.2 ml

        

        

3.2 Designation: Cylinders are designated by reference to ISO 11040-1 plus colour of the glass. Example: *Glass cylinders for dental local anaesthetic cartridges ISO 11040-1 – cl* (clear glass).

        

### 4 Requirements

        

4.1 Material: Colourless (cl) glass of hydrolytic resistance grain class ISO 720 HGA 1. Any change in chemical composition shall be notified to the user at least nine months in advance.

        

4.2 Performance Requirements:

        

            
- 4.2.1 Sealing surface at open end shall be flat and free from ripples

            
- 4.2.2 No seeds or bubbles that interfere with visual inspection of contents

            
- 4.2.3 No open air lines on inside surface that can lead to leakage

            
- 4.2.4 Hydrolytic resistance per ISO 4802-1 or ISO 4802-2, class HC 1

            
- 4.2.5 If annealed: maximum residual stress ≤ 40 nm/mm optical retardation

        

        

### 5 Marking

        

Outer packaging shall be marked with: size; quantity; designation; manufacturer name/symbol; lot number or date of production.

## Part 2: Part 4: Glass Barrels (Part 4)

# ISO 11040 Part 4: Glass Barrels for Injectables and Sterilized Subassembled Syringes Ready for Filling

    

    

ISO 11040-4:2015 | Third Edition

    

        

### 4 General Requirements

        

4.1 **Quality systems:** Activities within this part of ISO 11040 shall be carried out within a formal quality system. NOTE: ISO 15378 contains requirements for a suitable quality management system for primary packaging materials for medicinal products.

        

4.2 **Testing:**

        

            
- Gauge repeatability and reproducibility (GR&R) ≤ 20% of tolerance range for non-destructive tests; ≤ 30% for destructive tests, covering ±2 standard deviations.

            
- Sampling plans shall be based on statistically valid rationale (e.g. ISO 2859-1, ISO 3951).

            
- Testing at ambient laboratory conditions unless agreed otherwise.

        

        

4.3 **Documentation:**

        

            
- All compliance demonstrations shall be documented.

            
- Documentation retained considering regulatory requirements, expiration date, and traceability.

            
- Electronic records, signatures, and handwritten signatures on electronic records shall be reliable.

## Part 4: Part 6: Plastic Barrels (Part 6)

# ISO 11040 Part 6: Plastic Barrels for Injectables and Sterilized Subassembled Syringes Ready for Filling

    

    

ISO 11040-6:2019 | Second Edition

    

        

### 1 Scope

        

This document specifies materials, dimensions, quality, and performance requirements, as well as test methods for polymer barrels and sterilized subassembled syringes ready for filling, intended for single use only.

        

Prefilled syringes can be produced on dedicated and specifically designed processing equipment such as inline moulding and filling. This document does not apply but can be used also for such dedicated prefilled syringes.

        

### 3.1–3.8 Key Terms and Definitions

        

            
- **Customer:** Business entity purchasing syringe barrels or subassembled syringes for further processing/filling

            
- **Manufacturer:** Business entity manufacturing the polymer barrels or sterilized subassembled syringes

            
- **Staked needle syringe:** Syringe with a needle integrated into the barrel (by insert moulding, gluing, or other bonding)

            
- **Sterilized subassembled syringe ready for filling:** Subassembly pre-treated including injection moulding, needle assembly, inner surface lubrication, closure sealing, packaging (per ISO 11040-7), and sterilization

            
- **Sterile barrier system:** Minimum package preventing ingress of microorganisms; for tub-and-nest: formed by tub and sealing lid

        

        

### 4 General Requirements

        

4.1 **Quality systems:** Per formal quality system; ISO 15378 (primary packaging QMS) and ISO 13485 (medical device design control) both referenced.

        

4.2 **Testing:** GR&R ≤ 20% (non-destructive) or ≤ 30% (destructive); statistically valid sampling plans (ISO 2859-1, ISO 3951).

        

4.3 **Documentation:** All compliance documented; retention period considering regulatory requirements, expiration date, traceability. Electronic records shall be reliable.

## Part 5: Part 7: Packaging Systems (Part 7)

# ISO 11040 Part 7: Packaging Systems for Sterilized Subassembled Syringes Ready for Filling

    

    

ISO 11040-7:2015 | First Edition

    

        

### 1 Scope

        

This part of ISO 11040 specifies the packaging system used to deliver sterilized subassembled syringes ready for filling in tubs and nests. Downstream processes after filling are not within scope.

        

### 3 Terms and Definitions

        

            
- **Insert liner:** Foil to cover the filled nest

            
- **Nest:** Plastic plate with a defined hole pattern for the suspension of the syringe bodies

            
- **Tub:** Plastic container to accommodate the filled nest

            
- **Sealing lid:** Microbial barrier material for sealing the tub

            
- **Protective bag:** Plastic bag or sealing around the tub

            
- **Sterile barrier system:** Minimum package preventing ingress of microorganisms; for tub-and-nest: **formed by tub and sealing lid**

            
- **Packaging system:** Combination of sterile barrier system and protective packaging

        

        

### 4.1 General Requirements

        

4.1.1 The packaging system shall have acceptable microbiological and particulate levels to support introduction into aseptic filling environment. Requirements agreed by manufacturer and customer.

        

4.1.2 Materials, sterile barrier system, and packaging system enabling sterilization and maintaining sterility shall comply with ISO 11607-1. The protective bag, at minimum, protects tub from external contaminants; ideally also maintains sterility over shelf-life.

        

### 4.2 Nest Requirements

        

4.2.1 Information to be provided: external dimensions, deflection, holes for syringes, centering/lifting openings, free space for lifting tool engagement. Customer and manufacturer agree on dimensions and tolerances.

        

4.2.2 Design shall facilitate insertion and removal of sterilized subassembled syringes (e.g. Luer lock adapter syringes) by adding bevels or other means.

        

4.2.3 Maximum acceptable nest deflection agreed by manufacturer and customer; determined per Annex B.

### 4.3 Tub Requirements

        

4.3.2 Tub shall allow sealing of lid. Tub flange shall be free of sharp edges to protect packaging integrity.

        

4.3.3 If sterilization indicators applied to tubs, per ISO 11138 and ISO 11140.

        

### 4.4 Insert Liner

        

Shall release minimum particles. Where appropriate, permeable for sterilization agent (e.g. non-woven polyolefin). Can consist of several layers to shield glass against electron beam during decontamination. Layers shall be connected (e.g. by sealing). Shape and dimensions shall comply with tub.

        

### 4.5 Sealing Lid

        

Shall be sealable to tub and completely peelable from tub while minimizing particle release. Seal properties (strength, width) and integrity tested per validated test method (per ISO 11607-1). Shall be permeable for sterilization agent. Material selection shall consider decontamination processes (e.g. electron beam) prior to transfer into aseptic filling area.

        

### 4.6 Protective Bag

        

4.6.1 Permanently sealed; shall enable selected sterilization method. Seal properties tested per validated method (ISO 11607-1).

        

4.6.2 Can be single or double bag. For single bag: bag dimensions, sealing joint width/position/type, material, orientation of tub inside bag. For double bag: additional inner bag folding information required.

        

### 6 Marking of the Tub

        

Marked as agreed, can include: manufacturer name; article description; quantity of syringes; warning "do not use if packaging is damaged"; date of manufacture; manufacturer batch number; serial number. Machine-readable codes (barcodes, 2D codes) recommended. Symbols per ISO 7000 or ISO 15223-1.

## Part 6: Part 8: Finished Prefilled Syringes (Part 8)

# ISO 11040 Part 8: Requirements and Test Methods for Finished Prefilled Syringes

    

    

ISO 11040-8:2016 | First Edition

    

        

### 1 Scope

        

This part of ISO 11040 is applicable to aseptically filled or terminally sterilized finished prefilled syringes (intended for single use only) based on ISO 11040-4 or ISO 11040-6, together with ISO 11040-5, for parenteral injection preparations with focus on quality, functional performance and safety requirements.

        

NOTE 1: Can also be used as guidance for other types, designs and/or sizes (e.g. dual chamber prefilled syringes).

        

NOTE 2: If used in a needle-based injection system, see also ISO 11608-3.

        

NOTE 4: Finished prefilled syringes containing so-called borderline products (e.g. hyaluronic acid) are included, though not always regulated as pharmaceutical products.

        

### 3 Terms and Definitions

        

3.1 **Finished prefilled syringe:** *Prefilled container closure system for parenteral injection preparations as it is marketed, including e.g. assembly of components, terminal sterilization and final packaging.*

        

3.2 **Manufacturer:** *Natural or legal person holding the licence for the pharmaceutical product with responsibility for the design, manufacture, packaging, and labelling of a finished prefilled syringe, before it is placed on the market.*

        

### 4 User Requirements

        

4.1 **Definition of intended use:** Manufacturer shall define intended use considering:

        

            
- Users including their health status

            
- Route of administration

            
- Additional medical devices/application aids (needle-based injection systems, infusion tubes, plunger rod/finger flange)

            
- Characteristics of environment during transport, storage and use

            
- Point of use (hospital, homecare)

            
- Frequency of application; criticality of medication

        

        

4.2 **Risk management:** Manufacturers shall follow a risk-based approach per ISO 14971 during design, development, manufacture and life cycle. Risk management shall consider intended use, interactions between content and container, and environmental conditions. This can result in product-specific requirements and test methods that differ from this part of ISO 11040.

        

4.3 **Application of usability engineering:** The usability of the prefilled syringe shall be considered and validated according to a process compliant with IEC 62366. Instructions for use are part of usability testing.
